### Bullying Victimization and Youth Psychotic-Like Experiences: Evidence From a Longitudinal Discordant Twin and Sibling Design
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry (J Am Acad Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 10.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Karcher NR et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 19

OBJECTIVE: Childhood bullying victimization is a common and potentially modifiable stressor associated with psychotic-like experiences (PLEs), which confers risk for later psychiatric disorders. The present work examines reciprocal associations between bullying victimization and PLEs in youth, testing whether bullying victimization contributes to distressing PLEs independent of shared familial liability using longitudinal and genetically informed within-family analyses.

METHOD: Participants were 11,868 youth (baseline age 9-10 years) from 6 waves of the Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development (ABCD) Study across 21 U.S. sites. Any bullying victimization was assessed using both caregiver- and youth-report across multiple waves. Significantly distressing PLEs were defined as endorsement of ≥1 psychotic-like experience with high associated distress on the Prodromal Questionnaire-Brief Child Version. Random-intercept cross-lagged panel models tested bidirectional longitudinal associations. Genetically-informed analyses compared bullying-discordant twin and sibling pairs to distinguish individual-specific environmental effects from shared familial influences.

RESULTS: Bullying victimization was associated with increased odds of distressing PLEs (OR=3.62, 95% CI=3.31-3.96). Longitudinal models demonstrated reciprocal associations, with bullying victimization predicting subsequent PLEs and PLEs predicting later bullying victimization exposure. Critically, within-family analyses indicated evidence for both shared genetic (OR=6.87, 95% CI=5.37,8.78) and individual-specific contributions (OR=2.55, 95% CI=1.99,3.28) to the association between bullying victimization and significantly distressing PLEs.

CONCLUSION: Bullying victimization is associated with elevated risk for distressing psychotic-like experiences through both individual-specific environmental processes and shared familial liability. These findings provide evidence highlighting bullying prevention and early identification of affected youth as potential targets for reducing risk for later psychiatric disorders.

### Not all food avoidance is the same: a latent profile analysis informed by the Appetite Self-Regulation model
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry (J Child Psychol Psychiatry) *(IF: 7.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Arman O et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 19

BACKGROUND: Food avoidance is a common problem for some children and negatively affects their dietary quality. The Appetite Self-Regulation (ASR) model proposes that food avoidance phenotypes arise in children due to interactions between bottom-up and top-down processes. However, it is unknown how these factors combine within children to produce distinct food avoidant profiles. This study aimed to better understand different types of food avoidance by identifying food avoidant profiles in middle childhood and examining their neurodevelopmental correlates.

METHODS: Participants were 517 parents of children aged 5-12 years. Latent profile analysis was conducted using multiple ASR domains, including appetitive traits (satiety responsiveness, slowness in eating, food responsiveness, enjoyment of food and food fussiness), temperament traits (fear, shyness, inhibitory control, impulsivity), food avoidance behaviours (food avoidance, texture sensitivity and cognitive rigidity) and liking of low- and high-energy-density foods (vegetable and snack liking). Multinomial logistic regression examined associations between profile membership and neurodevelopmental disorders.

RESULTS: Five profiles were identified, including two food avoidant profiles. Multi-Domain Food Avoidant profile (n = 114, 22%) was characterised by a multi-domain form of avoidance, particularly showing higher levels of fear and shyness, suggesting greater bottom-up emotional reactivity to foods. The Rigid Texture-Sensitive Avoidant profile (n = 66, 13%) showed the highest texture sensitivity and cognitive rigidity, indicating a sensory-driven pathway to food avoidance. The Rigid Texture-Sensitive Avoidant profile was also characterised by the lowest inhibitory control, reflecting reduced top-down regulatory capacity. Children with neurodevelopmental disorders were disproportionately represented in the food avoidant profiles.

CONCLUSIONS: Food avoidance in middle childhood reflects heterogeneous behavioural patterns shaped by different combinations of bottom-up avoidance processes and top-down regulatory control. Identifying distinct avoidant profiles may improve understanding of the processes underlying children's food avoidance and inform more targeted approaches to supporting children with persistent avoidant eating behaviours.

### Child chaos: parent-reported sibling differences in chaos at home in association with externalising and internalising behaviours
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry (J Child Psychol Psychiatry) *(IF: 7.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Oliver BR, Midouhas E | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 20

BACKGROUND: Household chaos has long been associated with children's adjustment, relating to both externalising and-though somewhat less so-internalising behaviours. Original conceptualisations view chaos as an aspect of the home environment that may be shared by family members and/or differentially influence or be influenced by them. There is increasing recognition that home experiences can differ for siblings in ways important for their outcomes. To capture this for chaos, examining differential sibling perspectives has gained attention, but the extent to which parents perceive sibling differences in chaos and their relation to adjustment remains unstudied. In a preliminary study, for the first time, we distinguished family-wide and child-specific parent-reported chaos in association with sibling externalising and internalising behaviours.

METHODS: In a UK sample of 261 families with young sibling pairs (3-11 years; 522 children), we adapted the response format of the widely used measure, CHAOS, to assess parent reports of child chaos, that is, chaos 'for and around' each child in the home. Multilevel modelling examined family-wide (family average) and child-specific (child differentiation from the family average) chaos reported by mothers in the prediction of between- and within-family variances in externalising and internalising behaviours.

RESULTS: As hypothesised, accounting for key demographic and parenting confounders, family-wide chaos was associated with both externalising and internalising behaviours, whereas child-specific chaos was associated only with externalising behaviours.

CONCLUSIONS: Exploring parent perceptions of child chaos may offer key insights into associations between household chaos and young children's adjustment.

### Maternal thyroid function in the first half of pregnancy and child internalising and externalising problems over time
**כתב עת:** 🔷 European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry (Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 6.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Keestra SM et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 19

Adequate gestational thyroid function is important for offspring neurodevelopment, yet behavioural implications of variation within the normal range are unclear. We examined associations with externalising and internalising problems across repeated assessments in childhood and adolescence. 2,606 mother-child dyads from the Amsterdam Born Children and their Development (ABCD) cohort with maternal free thyroxine (FT4) and thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH) measurements in the first half of pregnancy and at least one child SDQ assessment. Primary analysis excluded women with overt thyroid disease or thyroid medication use and children born < 32 weeks or < 1500 g. Linear mixed-effects models were applied to Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire (SDQ) scores at 5-6, 11-12 and 15-16 years, via mother-, teacher-, and self-report. Secondary analyses included logistic regression for borderline SDQ cut-offs, sex interactions, and effect modification by TSH. FT4 showed a U-shaped association with teacher-reported externalising problems (quadratic β = 9.49, FDR-adjusted p = 0.03), with higher scores at both low and high FT4. Higher FT4 was associated with lower child-reported internalising problems (β = -0.17, FDR-adjusted p = 0.04). For both primary associations, there was no evidence of FT4-by-age interaction, suggesting differences in problem levels rather than thyroid-related differences in symptom change over time. At age 5-6, FT4 showed a non-linear association with teacher-reported hyperactivity/inattention above the threshold, with a negative linear term (OR = 0.83, 95% CI: 0.72 to 0.97) and a positive quadratic term (OR = 1.11, 95% CI: 1.02 to 1.20). At age 11-12, higher FT4 was associated with fewer self-reported peer problems (OR = 0.84, 95% CI:0.71-0.99). In exploratory analyses, curvature of the FT4-externalising association varied by maternal TSH; no sex modification was detected. Within a cohort free of overt thyroid disease, maternal thyroid function in the first half of pregnancy was associated with domain- and rater-specific differences in child internalising and externalising problems. We found no evidence that these associations changed across the available repeated assessments, suggesting differences in average problem scores rather than differences in how symptoms changed with age.

### Pornography use in adolescence: a gender perspective on co-occurring behavioural, psychosocial, and health outcomes
**כתב עת:** 🔷 European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry (Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 6.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Artazcoz L et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 19

This study examined associations between pornography use and gender identity, sexual attraction, sexual behaviours, substance use, adverse social experiences, and health and wellbeing among secondary school students from a gender perspective. This cross-sectional study used data from the 2025 FRESC Health Survey on Adolescence in Barcelona, Spain (3,690 students aged 13-19 years). Occasional or regular pornography use was contrasted with no use or one-time use. Sex-stratified survey-weighted complex-samples logistic regression models accounted for sampling strata and classroom clustering and were adjusted for age, family socioeconomic status, and family structure. Pornography use was more frequent among boys than girls (55.6% vs. 13.8%). Associations with ever having had sexual intercourse, daily smoking, recent drunkenness, lifetime cannabis use, peer aggression, and hyperactivity/inattention were observed in both sexes. Among girls, associations were additionally observed with transgender/nonbinary gender identity, non-exclusively heterosexual attraction, poor family relationships, peer victimization, sexual harassment, poor self-reported health, and life dissatisfaction; body dissatisfaction was significant only before correction for multiple testing. Among boys, conduct problems were also associated with pornography use. Following Benjamini-Hochberg correction across 21 outcomes per sex, seven of eight initially significant associations in boys and 13 of 14 in girls remained significant. Overall, these findings suggest that pornography use should not be understood as an isolated behaviour but rather in relation to a broader range of behavioural, psychosocial, and health characteristics during adolescence, although the cross-sectional design does not permit causal or temporal conclusions.
