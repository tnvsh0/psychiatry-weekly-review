### Surprise Resets the Mind: An Adaptive Online Reset in Working Memory Selection
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Psychological Science (Psychol Sci) *(IF: 5.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Yu J et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 20

Human cognition rapidly adapts to unexpected events in dynamic environments, but it remains unclear whether expectation violations rapidly influence the selection of attended information into working memory. Using the attribute-amnesia paradigm, we introduced expectation violations during the surprise trial to test whether unexpected changes improve reporting of a target feature that had been attended and used but was normally not reported. Across six experiments (total  N  = 250 college students, aged 17-27 years; 149 females), expectation violations reliably improved surprise-trial report of this feature, indicating greater availability in working memory. This effect occurred not only when the violation concerned the target, but also when it involved broader contextual or auditory changes, suggesting that the effect cannot be characterized simply as enhanced visual attention to the target. These findings suggest that expectation violations trigger a rapid, flexible adjustment of working-memory selection, highlighting its role in dynamically managing information under uncertainty.
