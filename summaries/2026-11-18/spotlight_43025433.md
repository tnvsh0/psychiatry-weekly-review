### Brain health in children born extremely preterm: a multidimensional perspective
**כתב עת:** ⭐ The Lancet Child & Adolescent Health (Lancet Child Adolesc Health) *(IF: 45.7)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר סקירה | **מחברים:** Padilla N et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 13

Gestational age and developmental stage of the brain at birth are major determinants of child health, and long-term consequences of preterm birth and altered brain maturation are inadequately understood simply through brain injury, survival rates, or diagnostic labels. In this Review, we conceptualise brain health in children born extremely preterm (before 28 weeks' gestation) as a developmental condition shaped by maturational timing, biological vulnerability, neonatal intensive care exposure, sensory and relational experiences, family circumstances, and social environments. We integrate evidence from research on early brain development and consciousness, long-term multidomain neurodevelopmental outcomes, neonatal intensive care practice, relational caregiving, early developmental interventions, and ethical, psychological, and social dimensions of care. Across these domains, extremely preterm birth emerges not as a single event but as a neuroplastic system that adapts to biological and environmental risks and protective factors that shape neurodevelopmental resilience and vulnerabilities and establish developmental trajectories. We argue that care for the infant born extremely preterm is optimised through a brain-health framework where care becomes a longitudinal, ethical process that integrates family support, minimises harmful exposures, promotes adaptive development, and fosters participation, wellbeing, and quality of life.
