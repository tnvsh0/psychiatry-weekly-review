INTRODUCTIONS: Lithium remains the first-line maintenance treatment for bipolar disorder (BD), yet its safety in older-age BD (OABD) is uncertain due to limited large-scale data. OABD patients often experience more severe physical comorbidities compared to younger BD patients, but systematic evidence regarding lithium's association with these conditions is lacking. This study examined the association between lithium use and physical comorbidities across age groups using the international Global Aging and Geriatric Experiments in Bipolar Disorder (GAGE-BD) dataset.

METHODS: A cross-sectional analysis was conducted using combined Wave 1 and 2 data from GAGE-BD project, encompassing 37 studies from 20 sites worldwide. Participants aged ≥ 18 years with BD were classified according to current lithium use. Physical comorbidities were harmonised into eight organ-system domains. Sociodemographic and clinical variables were compared between individuals receiving lithium treatment and those not prescribed lithium. Generalised linear mixed models adjusted for age, site and lithium × age interaction were applied to evaluate associations with physical comorbidities.

### The effect of psychosocial skill-oriented group psychoeducation on functional recovery, treatment adherence, and mental well-being in patients diagnosed with schizophrenia: A randomized controlled trial

BACKGROUND AND AIM: Schizophrenia is a chronic psychiatric disorder that negatively affects individuals' social, occupational, and daily functioning. Psychosocial skills-focused group psychoeducation has emerged as an essential complementary intervention method in clinical practice to increase patients' treatment adherence and enhance their mental well-being. This study examines its effects on functional improvement, treatment adherence, and mental well-being in schizophrenia patients.

METHODS: The study is a randomized controlled study with pre-test and post-test. This study was conducted with patients diagnosed with schizophrenia registered at the Community Mental Health Center of a hospital in Türkiye between June and August 2025. Participants were assigned to the experimental group (n 1  = 20) and the control group (n 2  = 20) by simple randomization from those who completed psychosocial skills-focused group psychoeducation. Data collection tools included the Participant Information Form, Morisky Medication Adherence Rating Scale, Warwick-Edinburgh Mental Well-being Scale, and Functional Recovery Scale in Schizophrenia. Descriptive statistics were used to analyze the data, and the Mann-Whitney U and Wilcoxon tests were used for data that did not follow a normal distribution.
