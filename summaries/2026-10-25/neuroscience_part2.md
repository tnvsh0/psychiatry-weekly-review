### Early lineage divergence segregates sensory and motor-associative identities in the thalamus
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Nature Neuroscience (Nat Neurosci) *(IF: 25.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Puche-Aroca L et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 20

The thalamus integrates sensory, motor and associative functions through distinct nuclei, yet the developmental principles driving their specification remain unclear. Here we generate a spatiotemporal single-cell multiomic and spatial transcriptomic atlas of the embryonic mouse thalamus, combined with barcoding-based lineage tracing. We identify two major glutamatergic lineages, sensory-related and motor-associative, each governed by different gene regulatory programs and temporal dynamics. These lineages arise from spatially and molecularly distinct progenitor domains, revealing an early segregation of functional identities. Within the sensory lineage, we resolve branching sensory trajectories leading to first-order visual and somatosensory neurons, both emerging from a shared higher-order-like state. Using in silico predictions and in vivo perturbation, we identify Sp9 as a key regulator of visual thalamic fate. Together, our findings define the molecular architecture and developmental trajectories that underpin thalamic modality specification, providing a framework to understand how functional circuits emerge in the mammalian brain.

### SIK3 controls synaptic localization of NMDA receptors by phosphorylating GluN1
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Nature Neuroscience (Nat Neurosci) *(IF: 25.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Lu R et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 21

Synaptic NMDA-type glutamate receptors (NMDARs) are central components of all neural computations. NMDAR levels critically vary between synapses but how the synaptic localization of NMDARs is controlled remains unclear. Here we show that SIK3, a serine-threonine protein kinase, localizes to synapses where it phosphorylates the obligatory NMDAR subunit GluN1 at a single site. This single phosphorylation event controls NMDAR recruitment to synapses. Genetic deletion of SIK3 severely and selectively decreases synaptic GluN1 levels by ~60% without altering AMPA-type glutamate receptor levels or inducing major transcriptional changes. Pharmacological inhibition of SIK3 impairs synaptic NMDAR responses within 45 minutes. Mutating the SIK3-phosphorylation site inhibits GluN1 synaptic recruitment. 'Sleepy', a gain-of-function mutation in SIK3 that increases sleep, selectively enhances synaptic NMDAR levels by increasing GluN1 phosphorylation. Thus, SIK3 phosphorylation of GluN1 provides a key signal for recruiting NMDARs to synapses.

### An acetylcholine-inhibited sleep-active hippocampal interneuron orchestrates memory consolidation
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Neuron *(IF: 17.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Feng Y et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 19

Memory reactivation during hippocampal ripples is critical for sleep-dependent memory consolidation. However, the molecular-to-circuit mechanism enabling sleep-predominant ripple generation remains unresolved. Here, we discovered that a sparse population of GABAergic interneurons in the mouse hippocampus specializes in gating ripple generation and memory consolidation. While all known genetically labeled hippocampal interneurons were highly active during wakefulness and rapid-eye-movement (REM) sleep, genetic targeting of M2 acetylcholine receptor (Chrm2)-expressing neurons isolated a specific population preferentially active during non-REM (NREM) sleep and quiet wakefulness. These acetylcholine-inhibited interneurons inhibited the entire hippocampus in an infra-slow rhythm (∼0.015 Hz). Suppressing their activity in dorsal CA3 or ventral hippocampus impaired spatial and social memory, respectively. Furthermore, Chrm2+ neurons were closely associated with ripples; their activity bidirectionally modulated ripple occurrence via widespread inhibition. Thus, Chrm2+ interneurons are critical in linking neuromodulatory state to memory consolidation, offering genetically targeted access to sleep-active memory circuits implicated in cognitive disorders.

### Prematurity insults remodel cerebellar development and behaviour
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Brain *(IF: 14.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Sanidas G et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 21

Preterm survivors often develop motor and socio-cognitive impairments that implicate altered cerebellar development, yet the underlying mechanisms remain poorly understood. A key challenge is that prematurity involves overlapping perinatal insults that converge on the developing brain, making their individual effects difficult to disentangle. Here, we model two major prematurity-associated insults, maternal immune activation (MIA) and neonatal hypoxia (Hx), in mice. By controlling timing and sequence, we define how these insults shape cerebellar assembly during rapid maturation. Comparative analysis with human tissue confirmed that these insults recapitulate key features of the human preterm cerebellum, establishing the translational validity of this model for dissecting insult-specific outcomes. Behavioral and kinematic profiling revealed divergent motor and social phenotypes, which we traced to insult-specific cerebellar remodelling. Hypoxia compromised both granule cell maturation in the internal granule layer and the principal excitatory afferents to Purkinje cells, a circuit state that manifested as impaired motor execution and stereotyped, sensory-disengaged social investigation. Maternal immune activation, by contrast, expanded the granule cell progenitor layer and reduced Purkinje cell dendritic complexity, a phenotype that preserved social preference but reorganized the kinematic structure of social investigation. When hypoxia followed maternal immune activation, it acted on this primed substrate to produce a distinct state in which the features established by prior inflammation were compounded by granule cell proliferative arrest, progressive mitochondrial dysfunction, and aberrant, hyper-interactive social investigation. Together, these findings reframe prematurity-associated insults as cerebellar reprogramming events shaped by both the identity and sequence of insults, linking distinct mechanistic substrates to divergent neurodevelopmental outcomes.

### The Roles and Uses of Extracellular Vesicles in Neuroimmunologic Diseases
**כתב עת:** 🔷 The Journal of Neuroscience (J Neurosci) *(IF: 6.7)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר סקירה | **מחברים:** Kammeyer R, Coughlan C | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 21

Neuroimmunology is a field of neurology focused on the diagnosis and treatment of diseases where the immune system causes inflammation or injury to the nervous system. This includes diseases such as multiple sclerosis, myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein antibody-associated disease, neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder, autoimmune encephalitis, autoimmune ataxia, stiff person syndrome spectrum disorder, and neuro-rheumatologic diseases such as neuropsychiatric lupus. Over the past couple decades, there have been significant advancements in the diagnostic methods for several neuroimmunologic diseases. Despite these achievements, major needs remain for additional methods to facilitate earlier diagnosis, monitor disease activity and treatment, and elucidate disease mechanisms to aid in the development of novel or tailored treatment strategies. Currently, there is often a trade-off between the invasiveness of neurodiagnostic testing and the level of detail that can be obtained from the test without causing an iatrogenic injury. Here, we discuss the potential of extracellular vesicles (EVs), especially CNS-derived EVs obtained from peripheral blood, as a promising avenue for advancing research and clinical care in neuroimmunology. This article provides a review of the key clinical features and approaches to care for the most common neuroimmunologic diseases. We identify gaps that remain in our current understanding of these conditions. We then highlight how ongoing research in the EV field may address these existing gaps. Finally, we examine the challenges that must be overcome before EV analysis can be more widely adopted in both research and clinical care in this field.
