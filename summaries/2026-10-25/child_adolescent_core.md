### Meta-Analytic Evidence of Clinical, Cognitive, Biological and Treatment Characteristics of Bipolar Spectrum Disorder in Children and Adolescents: Results From an Umbrella Review
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry (J Am Acad Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 10.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר סקירה | **מחברים:** Torres-Cortes J et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 20

OBJECTIVE: To comprehensively synthesize the meta-analytic evidence regarding bipolar spectrum disorder (BSD) in children and adolescents.

METHOD: PRISMA-compliant umbrella review (PROSPERO:CRD420250651065) across multiple databases to identify meta-analyses involving children and adolescents with BSD. Literature search, screening, data extraction, and quality assessment (via AMSTAR-2) were performed independently. Findings were synthesized and grouped according to key clinical, cognitive, biological and treatment domains.

RESULTS: Fifty-two meta-analyses were included (k=754, N=59,967, age=13.0 years, 44.8% female participants). Most reported symptoms were increased energy (79%, 95% confidence interval (CI)=61-93%), irritability (77%, 95%CI=64-88%), and mood lability (76%, 95%CI=55-92%). Psychiatric comorbidities were common, with attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder being most frequent (53%, 95%CI=36-70%). Treatment delay was longer in children and adolescents than in adults with BSD (SMD=0.39, 95%CI=0.15-0.64). 26.2% (95%CI=24.4-28.0) of children and adolescents with BSD attempted suicide at least once, with higher risk vs. children and adolescents with depression (OR=1.59; 95%CI=1.24-2.05) and adult-BSD (OR=1.68, 95%CI=1.29-2.18). Compared to healthy controls (HCs), children and adolescents with BSD showed neurocognitive impairments. Abnormalities in neuroimaging markers were significant in children and adolescents with BSD vs. HCs, children and adolescents with depression and adult-BSD. Second-generation antipsychotics (SMD=0.68, 95%CI=0.51-0.86) and mood stabilizers (SMD=0.61, 95%CI=0.40-0.82) were superior for mania vs. placebo. Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) was associated with improvements in manic and depressive symptoms.

CONCLUSION: BSD in children and adolescents is linked to illness severity, cognitive deficits, and neuroimaging abnormalities, requiring integrated pharmacological and psychosocial interventions (e.g. CBT). SGAs effectively treat mania, but options for depression remain limited. More prognostic and long-term studies are needed.

### Cariprazine in the Treatment of Pediatric Patients With Irritability Associated With Autism Spectrum Disorder: A Randomized Controlled Trial
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry (J Am Acad Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 10.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Findling RL et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 22

OBJECTIVE: To evaluate the efficacy and safety of cariprazine in youth with irritability associated with autism spectrum disorder (ASD).

METHOD: This 8-week, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, controlled trial compared age- and weight-dependent flexibly-dosed cariprazine (0.75-3 mg/day) versus placebo in youth (5-17y) with ASD and irritability. The primary efficacy endpoint was week 8 change from baseline in caregiver-rated Aberrant Behavior Checklist-Irritability subscale (ABC-I) score. The first key secondary endpoint was the week 8 proportion of participants who were responders for clinician-rated Clinical Global Impressions-Change (CGI-C) in irritability ("much improved" or "very much improved").

RESULTS: 155 participants (mean age 10.6y, 70% male participants) received ≥1 study dose; 152 had baseline and ≥1 postbaseline ABC-I or CGI-C scores (n=74 cariprazine, n=78 placebo), with 81% completing the treatment period and 3.9% discontinuing due to treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs). The cariprazine group had significantly greater week 8 ABC-I improvement than placebo (least square mean difference [95% CI] = -3.7 [-6.75, -0.57], Cohen's d effect size = 0.42, p = 0.0205). A significantly greater percentage of cariprazine-treated participants were CGI-C Irritability responders at week 8 than placebo (68.9% vs. 38.0%, odds ratio [95% CI] = 3.6 [1.7, 7.8], p = 0.0012). Overall, 51% of participants had TEAEs (1.3% had severe TEAEs); most commonly, somnolence and headache. TEAEs, laboratory values, and vital signs were consistent with cariprazine's known adult safety profile.

CONCLUSION: Pediatric patients with ASD treated with cariprazine demonstrated significantly greater improvement in irritability than placebo.

### Adverse childhood experiences and child executive functions: influences of deprivation and threat in a Brazilian birth cohort
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry (J Child Psychol Psychiatry) *(IF: 7.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Cardoso AS et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 21

BACKGROUND: A large proportion of children in low- and middle-income countries are exposed to adverse childhood experiences (ACEs), increasing the risk of poor physical and mental development. The Dimensional Model of Adversity and Psychopathology proposes that deprivation and threat have distinct effects on child executive functioning (EF), but longitudinal evidence, particularly in low- and middle-income countries, remains limited. We examined associations between deprivation, threat and their subtypes with child EFs in Brazil.

METHODS: We analysed data from over 2,000 children aged 4 and 6-7 years from a population-based birth cohort in Pelotas, Brazil. ACEs were assessed at age 4 using caregiver questionnaires and filmed observations. Composite exposure scores for deprivation, threat and their subtypes were generated using principal component analysis. Composite EF outcome scores were similarly derived from measures of inhibitory control, cognitive flexibility and working memory at ages 4 and 6-7. Associations were estimated using adjusted linear regression models.

RESULTS: Total deprivation was associated with lower EF scores at both ages, as were separate indices of material and interactional deprivation. Children in the highest versus lowest tertile of total deprivation had EF scores 0.67 standard deviations lower at age 6-7 years (β = -0.67; 95% CI: -0.78, -0.56). Total threat (highest versus lowest tertile: β = -0.28; 95% CI: -0.39, -0.17; 6-7 anos) and direct threat were also associated with lower EF scores at both ages, whereas household threat showed no evidence of association.

CONCLUSIONS: Deprivation and threat in early childhood were both associated with lower executive function scores at school entry, with material deprivation and direct threat showing the strongest associations within their respective domains. These findings extend the Dimensional Model of Adversity and Psychopathology and highlight the importance of socioeconomic and trauma-informed strategies to support children exposed to adversity.

### Glymphatic dysfunction in early-onset schizophrenia: DTI-ALPS index reduction tracks illness burden and is independently associated with cognitive performance in treatment-resistant patients
**כתב עת:** 🔷 European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry (Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 6.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Oz A et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 22

Early-onset schizophrenia (EOS) is associated with greater cognitive impairment than adult-onset disease, and many patients also develop treatment resistance. Glymphatic dysfunction has been described in adult schizophrenia but not in EOS or in relation to treatment resistance. We used diffusion tensor imaging along the perivascular space (DTI-ALPS) to assess glymphatic function in 30 adolescents with EOS (15 treatment-resistant, TRS; 15 non-treatment-resistant, nTRS) and 29 age- and sex-matched healthy controls (HC). Participants completed a neuropsychological battery covering verbal memory, processing speed, executive function, working memory, verbal fluency, and social cognition. ALPS differed across groups (F(2,56) = 34.66, p < 0.001, η² = 0.553), with values decreasing from HC (1.625 ± 0.072) to nTRS (1.501 ± 0.098) to TRS (1.439 ± 0.048); the TRS-nTRS difference was not significant (d = 0.81, p = 0.066). Patients showed broad cognitive deficits relative to HC, but nTRS and TRS did not differ on any measure after FDR correction. ALPS showed a nominal inverse association with the number of psychotic episodes (ρ = -0.391, p = 0.033) but was unrelated to PANSS scores or antipsychotic dose. Within TRS, ALPS was independently associated with phonemic verbal fluency (COWAT: β = +0.74, p = 0.003); a parallel association with categorical fluency (β = +0.88, p < 0.001) was partly driven by an influential observation and is exploratory. Glymphatic dysfunction appears to be a disease-intrinsic feature of EOS, lowest in TRS, possibly linked to cumulative episodes rather than current symptoms, and may contribute to cognitive impairment in treatment-resistant patients.

### Mental health-related service contact amongst young people with a possible eating problem in the English national child mental health surveys
**כתב עת:** 🔷 European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry (Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 6.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Geers B et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 17

OBJECTIVE: To describe patterns of service contact among young people screening positive for possible eating problems and their characteristics.

METHODS: Secondary analysis of the Mental Health of Children and Young People in England 2017 survey, a national stratified probability sample. Possible eating problems were identified using Development and Wellbeing Assessment (DAWBA) eating disorder screening items in young people aged 11-19. Individuals answered questions regarding contact with sources of help for any mental health concern.

RESULTS: Overall, 36.4% of young people screened positive for a possible eating problem, of whom 60.7% reported no contact with sources of mental health support. Among those reporting contact with formal and specialist services, a substantial proportion screened positive for possible eating problems, including half of those reporting contact with mental health specialists. In weighted multinomial logistic regression restricted to screen-positive young people, older age (17-19 years) and presence of a DSM-5 diagnosis on the DAWBA were associated with higher likelihood of contact across informal, formal, and secondary healthcare sources. Young people from minoritised ethnic backgrounds were less likely to report secondary healthcare contact than White peers.

CONCLUSION: Despite high numbers of young people screening positive for possible eating problems, rates of help seeking for any mental health concerns in this group were low. Within this subgroup, service contact varies by age, ethnicity, and psychiatric morbidity. Differences in service contact in this subgroup should be further explored.

### Early predictors of the childhood dysregulation profile from ages 5 to 8: evidence from the UK household study
**כתב עת:** 🔷 European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry (Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 6.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Vitkauskaite E, Bilgin A | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 22

Childhood dysregulation profile (DP) involves co-occurring difficulties regulating emotions, behaviour and cognitions, and is associated with adverse outcomes. However, its early predictors are not well understood. We examined child- and family-related predictors of borderline and clinical DP symptoms at ages 5 and 8 years. Participants were 3,467 children from the UK Household Longitudinal Study. Parents reported on early child-related factors (infant regulatory problems, age 3 temperament and screen media use), prenatal exposures (smoking, alcohol) and postnatal family factors (parental psychological distress, stimulating home environment), and DP symptoms at ages 5 and 8 years. Hierarchical logistic regression analyses were conducted to examine predictors of borderline and clinical DP symptoms and their persistence over time. Negative affect at age 3 predicted borderline and clinical DP symptoms at ages 5 and 8 years, as well as symptom persistence. Other child-related predictors included impulsivity, impatience and irregularity, whereas family-related factors were not consistent predictors. These findings highlight the role of child temperament, particularly negative affect, in the development and persistence of dysregulation symptoms. Early identification of temperament-based risk factors may support targeted interventions to strengthen children's self-regulation skills before dysregulation difficulties become more persistent and difficult to modify.
