### Ties That Bind, Places That Matter: The Life-Satisfaction Gap Between Partnered and Single Individuals Varies Across Regions
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Psychological Science (Psychol Sci) *(IF: 5.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Gonzalez Avilés T et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Aug 24

People in romantic relationships often report higher life satisfaction than single individuals. We propose that this life-satisfaction gap reflects, in part, alignment with the prevailing norm of being in a relationship. In a preregistered study with a large-scale data set ( N  = 382,078 adults across 379 regions in Great Britain), we replicated prior findings: partnered (vs. single) individuals reported higher life satisfaction. Notably, this life-satisfaction gap varied by region, being larger in regions with higher relationship rates and smaller in regions with lower relationship rates. This effect persisted after we controlled for various confounds; it passed robustness checks and replicated at a smaller scale across 218 London neighborhoods. Together, our findings emphasize the importance of situating relationship experiences within their geographic contexts, offering new insights into where and why differences in life satisfaction between partnered and single individuals emerge.

### The WHO Said What? Interaction of Source Credibility and Readers' Prior Beliefs in the Reading of Social-Media Posts
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Psychological Science (Psychol Sci) *(IF: 5.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Virtanen OJ, Kaakinen JK | **תאריך:** 2026 Aug 25

Evaluating sources is an essential skill in our misinformation-riddled media environment. However, previous studies suggest that people tend not to engage with source information during reading, and very little research exists on how source credibility and readers' beliefs interact on social media. We examined how source credibility and prior beliefs affect reading social-media posts. Finnish university students ( N  = 83) first filled out a questionnaire measuring beliefs on eight controversial topics and then read 76 mock-up tweets on these topics while their eye movements were recorded. The sources of the tweets were pretested to be either credible or noncredible sources for the topic. Eye-movement results indicate that when credible sources say something belief-inconsistent, or if noncredible sources something belief-consistent, reading of the tweet is slower, and more attention is paid to the sources. Findings suggest that these "source-belief" inconsistencies disrupt reading and elicit deeper processing of source information.

### Object perception encodes mutually exclusive possibilities
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of Experimental Psychology: General (J Exp Psychol Gen) *(IF: 5.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Brody G et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Sep

Human adults represent and reason about possibilities: multiple, mutually exclusive, nonactual ways the world could be. It is debated whether this is a late-developing, human-specific capacity and whether it requires natural language. We propose that instead of a unitary high-level capacity, multiple cognitive capacities might independently represent possibilities in different ways and with different properties. Our case study is object perception, a capacity shared with infants and nonhuman animals. Using methods that tap specifically into visual processes, across four experiments (N = 160), we found possibility representations in vision tasks that do not involve high-level reasoning or language. In Experiments 1 and 2, we found evidence for the visual encoding of possible object identities: When there were two possible ways that features might map onto visual objects, both possible mappings were primed. In Experiment 3, we found that visual feature binding is a necessary precondition for such priming, ruling out low-level associative and high-level reasoning-based alternatives. Finally, in Experiment 4, we discovered that the visual system cannot encode one visual object possibly being at two possible locations. This pattern helps to explain a host of previous findings from the comparative and developmental literature and suggests that the unit of analysis for possibility representations should be the cognitive capacity rather than the agent. ### Task similarity increases difficulty in maintaining but not in switching between tasks
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of Experimental Psychology: General (J Exp Psychol Gen) *(IF: 5.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Poncet M | **תאריך:** 2026 Sep

Switching tasks and adapting to new situations by appropriately allocating mental resources is crucial for interacting with the world around us. This ability requires cognitive control and includes several operations such as maintaining and modifying task goals, allocating attention to the relevant stimulus, and selecting the appropriate motor command. Surprisingly, although we know that switching between tasks incurs costs, whether the type of activity itself makes any difference has rarely been investigated. This study uses an innovative, well-controlled paradigm that keeps response rules and stimulus set constant across tasks to specifically test how efficiently humans switch between tasks sharing similar versus dissimilar neural processes. We assessed the efficiency of transient and global cognitive control by comparing participants' switch costs, the efficiency of task reconfiguration, and mixing costs, the efficiency of task maintenance. In four experiments, employing different stimuli and tasks, the results consistently show that increased similarity between pairs of tasks increased the difficulty in maintaining the tasks but not in switching between them. This dissociation confirms that switch and mixing costs are independent processes. Importantly, the overlap in neural processing between tasks appears to be detrimental in simultaneously maintaining tasks, potentially due to competition or interference. Distinct task sets are more efficient to maintain than similar ones. (PsycInfo Database Record (c) 2026 APA, all rights reserved).
