### Mortgage Lending Risk Maps From the 1930s Influence the Prevalence of Youth Mental Health Problems in the Modern Era
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry (J Am Acad Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 10.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Burt SA et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 9

OBJECTIVE: Historical mortgage lending policies and practices serve as a key foundation of modern-day socioeconomic systems. Few of these structural legacies have been as powerful as those for 20 th  century mortgage lending risk maps - often referred to as redlining. Although extant studies have identified mental health disparities among residents of formerly redlined neighborhoods, the legacies of greenlined or otherwise positively designated neighborhoods are largely unknown. This represents a key gap in the literature, as efforts to illuminate the historical legacies of these maps must also include the ways in which they may have cemented concentrated advantage and prosperity.

METHOD: The current study sought to fill this gap in a population-based sample of 20,222 children and adolescents in 10,111 families, recruited via birth records into the Michigan State University Twin Registry. We fitted a series of multilevel models to illuminate the full legacy of 20 th  century mortgage lending risk maps for youth mental health in the 21 st  century.

RESULTS: Multilevel models indicated that, as expected, youth residing in formerly redlined neighborhoods (those designated as "hazardous") exhibited more symptomatology than youth residing in other types of neighborhoods. However, the most prominent and persistently observed differences were found for youth in formerly greenlined neighborhoods (those designated as "best"), who exhibited substantially better mental health than youth residing in any other type of neighborhood. What's more, the effects of greenlining persisted even when controlling for neighborhood and familial socioeconomic factors, indicating that these variables do not account for the observed effects.

CONCLUSION: Such findings serve to further highlight both the unfair legacies of disadvantage and the entrenched legacies of prosperity wrought by historical mortgage lending risk maps.

### Efficacy and acceptability of digital safety planning therapy for self-injurious thoughts and behaviors in adolescents with depressive episodes: a randomized controlled trial
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry (J Child Psychol Psychiatry) *(IF: 7.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Liu YH et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 9

BACKGROUND: Self-injurious thoughts and behaviors (SITB) represent a significant mental health issue among Chinese adolescents. Research on smartphone-based safety planning as digital therapeutics for SITB in China remains limited. This randomized controlled trial evaluated the efficacy and acceptability of a safety planning mobile application (LIFES) for reducing SITB in adolescents with depressive episodes.

METHODS: Among 189 adolescents with depressive episodes screened, 122 were randomized to either the intervention group (LIFES app plus treatment as usual [TAU], n = 61) or the control group (TAU only, n = 61). Follow-up assessments at 1, 3, and 6 months measured SITB, depression, anxiety, and sleep quality. This trial is registered with the Chinese Clinical Trial Registry (ChiCTR2300075663; https://www.chictr.org.cn/).

RESULTS: Generalized linear mixed models showed significant time-by-group interaction effects for non-suicidal self-injury frequency (F = 3.39, p = .018), suicide planning seriousness (F = 2.93, p = .034), confidence in suicide completion during preparation (F = 3.10, p = .027), suicide threat/gesture frequency (F = 3.64, p = .013), and total suicidal behavior frequency (F = 3.31, p = .020) after adjusting for depression and anxiety symptom severity, sleep quality, and years of education. The intervention group showed significantly greater reductions in SITB characteristics compared to the control group. User experience surveys indicated good satisfaction with the application.

CONCLUSIONS: This study demonstrates that safety planning-based digital therapeutics are an effective adjunctive intervention for SITB in adolescents with depressive episodes. Future research should examine the applicability of this intervention to broader populations.

### The emotion regulation and recognition account of empathy-related processes in autism: a multimodal, multilevel case-control study
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry (J Child Psychol Psychiatry) *(IF: 7.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Wood JJ et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 10

BACKGROUND: Recent evidence suggests that many autistic individuals exhibit heightened emotional responsiveness to others' affect despite greater difficulty recognizing others' emotions and accurately inferring their perspectives. This study introduces and preliminarily evaluates the Emotion Regulation and Recognition Account (ERRA), a model proposing that susceptibility to emotion dysregulation and challenges with emotion cue interpretation jointly shape empathy-related processing in autism.

METHODS: Children aged 6-13 years (N autistic  = 103; N non-autistic  = 40) participated; groups were matched demographically. Children watched five videos of youth exhibiting strong affect (e.g. sad and angry) and answered questions about their reactions to and understanding of the videos. These responses were coded for empathy-related processes such as emotional contagion and perspective taking. Electrodermal reactivity to and eye gaze towards the videos were also measured. Multilevel models were employed to test hypothesized pathways in the ERRA.

RESULTS: Autistic children experienced greater affective intensity but exhibited less accurate emotion recognition than non-autistic children when observing youth exhibiting strong affect. Greater physiological and affective intensity were associated with greater attention shifting, and more frequent attention shifting among autistic children was associated with less accurate emotion recognition. Less accurate emotion recognition was, in turn, associated with less emotional synchrony and perspective taking, which were linked with less prosocial motivation.

CONCLUSIONS: The findings provide initial support for the ERRA by suggesting that, rather than experiencing indifference, many autistic individuals have strong affective reactions when observing others in distress. These reactions may lead to attention shifting, which can reduce awareness of others' emotional cues, potentially interrupting the unfolding of empathic understanding and responding. Empathy-supporting interventions can build upon the affective responsiveness to others experienced by many autistic individuals.

### Links of fitbit-derived sleep architecture and fragmentation with ADHD symptoms and externalizing behaviors in adolescents
**כתב עת:** 🔷 European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry (Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 6.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Clapham C et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 9

Sleep disturbances are linked to adolescent externalizing behaviors and attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) symptoms. However, few studies have examined associations between objectively measured sleep architecture and these behavioral outcomes. This study investigated whether specific sleep stages and fragmentation are associated with externalizing behaviors and ADHD symptoms in a large U.S. population-based cohort. Participants were 4,545 adolescents (48.4% female; 73% White) from the Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development (ABCD) study with available Fitbit sleep data and parent-reported behavioral assessments. Sleep predictors included absolute duration and proportional time spent in rapid eye movement (REM), deep, and light sleep, as well as wake after sleep onset (WASO). Outcomes were externalizing behaviors and ADHD symptoms. Models adjusted for annual household income, sex, age, race, and weekend status. Lower REM sleep, both in absolute duration and relative proportion, was significantly associated with higher ADHD symptoms but not with externalizing behaviors after correction for multiple testing. Greater sleep fragmentation, indexed by longer absolute and relative WASO, was associated with elevated ADHD symptoms and, less robustly, externalizing behaviors. Light and deep sleep were not significantly associated with either outcome. REM sleep deficiency was associated with ADHD symptoms, whereas sleep fragmentation was associated with ADHD symptoms and, less robustly, with externalizing behaviors. Associations were cross-sectional; longitudinal and experimental studies are needed to determine whether these sleep characteristics contribute to, or could be targeted to improve, behavioral outcomes in adolescents.

### The mental health sequelae of mass shootings on affected children, adolescents and young adults - a scoping review
**כתב עת:** 🔷 European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry (Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 6.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר סקירה | **מחברים:** Böge J et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 9

While the incidence of school shootings is highest in the US worldwide, these events also occur in Europe, such as the recent fatal school shooting in Graz in Austria, highlighting the importance of evidence-based care for the survivors. This study aims to synthesize existing evidence on the short- and long-term effects of mass shootings on the mental health of children, adolescents, and young adults, and to identify risk factors that predispose individuals to psychological sequelae to inform appropriate aftercare recommendations. After conducting a literature search in PubMed, Medline, Scopus and the Cochrane Library, 483 references were identified, which were screened by two independent raters according to inclusion criteria, resulting in 15 eligible studies. Most shootings examined took place in the US, with some focusing on shootings in Finland. All studies examined either adolescents or young adults. No studies examining the effects on children were found. The results showed that directly after such an event, a large proportion of the affected show posttraumatic stress symptomatology (PTSS); in the long-term most recuperate without intervention, however a small percentage remain symptomatic and later develop PTSD. Key risk factors include female sex, younger age, previous traumatic experiences, an avoidant attachment style and a higher grade of exposure. Social support is an important protective factor. Based on the findings, a multitiered approach tailored to the needs of affected individuals is recommended, supported by a coordinated screening process to identify those with relevant risk factors.

### Hallucinations associated with bilastine in a teenager: case report and disproportionality analysis in VigiBase(®)
**כתב עת:** 🔷 European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry (Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 6.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Ewig E et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 8

Bilastine is a second-generation H1-antihistamine considered to have minimal central nervous system penetration and low anticholinergic activity. Neuropsychiatric adverse drug reactions (ADRs), including hallucinations, are not expected with bilastine, although such reactions have been reported with other second-generation antihistamines (sgAH). We report the case of a 13-year-old girl who developed visual hallucinations and panic attacks shortly after starting treatment with bilastine, after having experienced a recurrent sensation of presence while taking desloratadine. The patient's symptoms resolved progressively after discontinuation of bilastine, with a Naranjo score of 6 indicating probable causality. VigiBase ®  analysis was also performed and identified a possible association between bilastine and hallucinations. This case, supported by pharmacovigilance data, although predominantly in older adults, suggests a potential pharmacovigilance signal with possible association between bilastine and hallucinations. Furthermore, similar neuropsychiatric ADRs have been reported with other second-generation antihistamines. Clinicians should be aware of this rare but potentially distressing ADR to ensure prompt recognition and discontinuation of treatment.
