### Chronic progressive demyelination induces a microglial state with lysosomal dysfunction
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Nature Neuroscience (Nat Neurosci) *(IF: 25.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Berghoff SA et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 26

Dysfunctional immune responses impair remyelination and contribute to neurodegeneration. While immune and metabolic responses to acute myelin injury are well characterized, myeloid cell adaptations to chronic myelin damage remain poorly understood. Here we performed a comparative analysis of genetic and toxic mouse models of acute and chronic demyelination, which identified a distinct microglial state associated with chronic demyelination, characterized by sustained pro-inflammatory activation, lysosomal lipid sequestration and reduced lipid droplet formation. Transcriptomics profiling further revealed a metabolic program marked by upregulated cholesterol synthesis and diminished sterol efflux. Treatment with a desmosterol mimetic possessing 24-dehydrocholesterol reductase inhibitor activity reversed this microglial state, attenuated pro-inflammatory signaling (including C-C Motif Chemokine Ligand 4 expression), reduced myelin damage and improved motor performance in a mouse model of leukodystrophy. Our findings uncover a pro-inflammatory microglial state characterized by lysosomal dysfunction and increased myelin reactivity, highlighting a novel therapeutic target for chronic demyelinating disorders.

### Trait-linked brainstem circuit resilience balances tau pathology-induced cellular vulnerability in Alzheimer's disease
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Neuron *(IF: 17.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Zheng JQ et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 27

Although tau pathology correlates with cognitive decline in Alzheimer's disease (AD), some individuals exhibit resilience by maintaining cognitive function. The mechanisms enabling this protection, particularly before widespread cortical degeneration occurs, remain elusive. Here, we identify a dynamic interplay between cellular vulnerability and circuit-level resilience that shapes cognitive outcomes in AD. Tau accumulation in the Edinger-Westphal nucleus (EW) impairs attention by inducing hypoexcitability in CART +  neurons via Kcnma1 upregulation, a vulnerability predictive of future decline in both 3×Tg-AD mice and AD patients. Conversely, a subset of AD mice with heightened exploratory behavior activates a compensatory medial parabrachial (mPB)-EW circuit, mediated by substance P-Tacr1 signaling, which sustains CART +  neuron function and delays cognitive deterioration. These findings reveal resilience as an active state enabled by trait-linked circuit plasticity that balances cellular vulnerability, defining multilevel targets for early intervention.

### Glioma regulates GABAergic signaling to drive hyperactivity and tumor progression
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Neuron *(IF: 17.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** McDonald MF et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 27

Recent studies have identified bi-directional communication between neurons and glioma tumors as a key driver of hyperexcitability and malignant progression. Although much of this work has focused on the activity of excitatory neurons, the roles of inhibitory neurons remain poorly defined. Using in vivo screening approaches in autochthonous glioma models, we found that the loss of a γ-aminobutyric acid (GABA) transporter (Slc6a1) enhanced glioma progression, promoted network hyperactivity, and influenced tonic GABA release by tumors. Mechanistically, we show that glioma tumors influence the activity of inhibitory neurons and that their subsequent stimulation suppresses tumor growth. Further screening of these phenomena identified diazepam binding inhibitor (DBI), a secreted negative allosteric modulator of GABA-A, as a link between glioma-related hyperexcitability and proliferation that functions through modulating inhibitory neuronal signaling. Collectively, our findings indicate that glioma tumors utilize multiple mechanisms to manipulate the activity of inhibitory neurons as a means to drive network hyperactivity, which ultimately facilitates malignant progression.

### The TBK1-OTUB2-IRF3 signalling complex promotes neuroinflammation and neurodegeneration
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Brain *(IF: 14.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Zhang J et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 29

The cyclic GMP-AMP synthase (cGAS)-stimulator of interferon genes (STING) pathway-mediated chronic inflammation drives neurodegeneration in natural aging and various neurodegenerative diseases, including amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). Here, we identify Otubain-2 (OTUB2) as a pivotal regulator that orchestrates the STING signalosome to amplify inflammatory responses. The function of OTUB2 in neuroinflammation and neurodegeneration was investigated through genetic deletion and pharmacological inhibition of OTUB2 in hTARDBP transgenic mice​, adeno-associated virus-mediated TDP43 overexpression mice, and aged mice. Biochemical assays were performed to dissect underlying molecular mechanisms. OTUB2 inhibited K63 ubiquitination of TANK-binding kinase 1 (TBK1), thereby suppressing TBK1 kinase activity and promoting cell death mediated by hyperactivated receptor-interacting serine/threonine-protein kinase 1 (RIPK1). Additionally, OTUB2 functioned as a scaffold facilitating assembly of the TBK1-interferon regulatory factor 3 (IRF3) signalling complex, a prerequisite for IRF3 phosphorylation, and enhanced IRF3 dimerization by reducing its K11 ubiquitination. Genetic deletion or pharmacological inhibition of OTUB2 significantly ameliorated neuroinflammation and neurodegeneration in both ALS and aged mouse models. This study establishes OTUB2 as a critical regulator of cGAS-STING signalling and a potential therapeutic target for neuroinflammation and neurodegeneration.

### Perception of tones versus tone streams under random multi-tone masking and its representation in auditory cortex
**כתב עת:** 🔷 The Journal of Neuroscience (J Neurosci) *(IF: 6.7)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Kistenmacher F et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 29

When repeated tones are presented among random masker tones, their perceptual organization is often variable. In some trials, they cannot be segregated from the masker tones. In other trials, often with a delay, they form coherent streams, segregated from the masker tones. A strong awareness-related negativity is evoked by each segregated tone of the stream in auditory cortex. A similar negative-going response has been observed when subtle, isolated tones are detected versus missed, raising the question of whether target tones that do not form a stream under informational masking are still perceived or not. Here a paradigm is introduced to probe this question while activity in auditory cortex was recorded with magnetoencephalography. Human listeners (13 female, 8 male) were instructed to indicate when they detected an isochronous stream of tones under multi-tone masking while listening to the stimulus. Thereafter, they indicated if a cue tone, presented subsequently to each trial, was comprised in the target and masker combination. Results confirmed that target tones could still be perceived in the subset of trials where they did not form a coherent stream, but the detectability in the cued frequency report was higher for tones forming a stream. A prominent awareness-related negativity was only observed when the tones formed a stream, suggesting that this response reflects the perceptual organization of the tones into streams and beyond the pure perception of the tones themselves. We speculate that the perceptual organization into a stream further enhances the detectability of the tones comprised in the stream. Significance Statement  Tone sequences may form streams that segregate from competing aspects of the auditory scene, a phenomenon considered comparable to visual object grouping. In multi-tone masking, hearing out streams of repeated tones seemingly enhances tone detectability by a degree that makes it difficult to judge whether these tones could at all be perceived if they had not formed a coherent stream. We present a paradigm that allows for probing both, the perception of target-tone frequency and the formation of coherent streams in the same trials. Applying this setup in magnetoencephalography showed that the perceptual organization into a stream is more strongly represented in auditory cortex than the ability to correctly detect a tone frequency within a random tone masker.
