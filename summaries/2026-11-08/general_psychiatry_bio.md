### Serum selenium, iron and rubidium are altered in treatment-resistant schizophrenia
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Molecular Psychiatry (Mol Psychiatry) *(IF: 13.4)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Luza S et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 2

The molecular basis of schizophrenia is complex, involving an interplay of genetic and environmental factors. In recent postmortem and imaging studies, we identified disturbances in iron homeostasis in cortical and subcortical regions of individuals with schizophrenia. These may be relevant to neuropathology identified in the disorder and could relate to mechanisms of oxidative stress and inflammation. Recent work has proposed that there is a dysregulation of brain metals and other elements in schizophrenia that might impact on oxidative stress. Using a metallomics approach, we report robust changes in serum levels of pro-oxidant iron, anti-oxidant selenium, and the potassium-marker, rubidium, in a cohort of people with treatment-resistant schizophrenia (TRS). The cohort also expressed elevated levels of serum pro-inflammatory cytokines and hepcidin, and lower levels of anti-oxidant selenoproteins, selenoprotein-P1 (SEPP1) and glutathione peroxidase 3 (GPX3). The changes in serum iron, rubidium and selenium discriminate between control individuals and TRS cases, and they were not affected by age or clozapine serum levels. We propose that the changes in selenium and cytokines might drive the expression of hepcidin, resulting in reduced serum iron levels. Further research should investigate the relationship between peripheral and central measures of these elements and their metabolism, and further explore the relevance of pro-oxidant stress to the neuropathology of schizophrenia.

### A Crh-defined insular cortex-brainstem-thalamic circuit regulates anxiety-related behavioral states
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Molecular Psychiatry (Mol Psychiatry) *(IF: 13.4)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** An ZY et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 30

Anxiety disorders are among the most prevalent and debilitating psychiatric conditions, yet the neural circuits underlying anxiety-related states remain incompletely understood. Here we identify a cortical-brainstem-thalamic pathway in which Crh-expressing neurons in the anterior and middle insular cortex (a/mIC) engage the parabrachial nucleus (PBN), which in turn recruits the posterior paraventricular thalamic nucleus (pPVT) to contribute to anxiety-related behavior. Single-cell calcium imaging reveals that the predator odorant 2-methyl-2-thiazoline (2-MT) and footshock recruit partially overlapping populations of PBN-projecting a/mIC neurons; neurons activated by both stimuli exhibit increased activity during open-arm approach in the elevated plus maze. Exposure to 2-MT increases corticotropin-releasing factor (CRF) signals in the PBN, where CRF enhances PBN neuronal excitability and CRF receptor 1 (CRFR1) signaling facilitates a/mIC-terminal-evoked firing. Inhibition of Crh⁺ a/mIC-PBN projections, Crh knockdown in the a/mIC, or blockade of CRFR1 signaling within the PBN attenuates 2-MT-induced anxiety-like behavior. Human neuroimaging further reveals stronger functional coupling within a putative homologous aIC-PBN-pPVT network in individuals with anxiety symptoms, and the strength of this coupling correlates with anxiety severity. Together, these findings define a molecularly specified insular circuit that contributes to anxiety-related behavioral states.

### A preconceptional stress model identifies thalamostriatal substrates of intergenerational vulnerability
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Molecular Psychiatry (Mol Psychiatry) *(IF: 13.4)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Iachizzi M et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 4

Antenatal depression affects up to 20% of pregnancies worldwide and is associated with increased neuropsychiatric vulnerability in offspring, yet the enduring behavioural, neuroanatomical, and molecular features associated with maternal mood disturbance remain incompletely characterized. To model depression that begins before conception, female mice were subjected to social-isolation rearing (SIR) from weaning through gestation and lactation, and behavioural, neuroanatomical, and molecular outcomes were assessed in adult offspring of both sexes. Offspring of SIR dams exhibited robust anxiety-like behaviours across multiple paradigms. High-resolution ex vivo structural magnetic resonance imaging revealed reduced relative volumes of the thalamus and striatum, together with relative expansion of gustatory/visceral and visual cortices. Multivariate analyses identified a pattern of thalamostriatal and other regional volume differences that covaried with behavioural impairment. Diffusion tensor imaging further identified region-specific elevations in fractional anisotropy, consistent with persistent differences in brain microstructure. Transcriptomic profiling of the thalamus revealed widespread, sex-dependent differences in gene expression, implicating pathways related to synaptic organization, protein synthesis, neuronal plasticity, and myelination. Exploratory DNA-methylation analyses identified nominal, sex-dependent pathway enrichments that did not survive false-discovery-rate correction. Together, these findings show that maternal stress beginning before pregnancy leaves a persistent imprint on offspring brain structure, microstructure, and epigenetic regulation, converging on the thalamus as a central hub of intergenerational vulnerability. This work establishes SIR as a translationally relevant model of depression that predates conception and provides integrated MRI- and molecular-level biomarkers for dissecting mechanisms and informing early preventive strategies.

### Dynamic epigenetic changes during antidepressant pharmacotherapy in major depressive disorder
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Molecular Psychiatry (Mol Psychiatry) *(IF: 13.4)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Müller S et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 2

Although antidepressants remain the main pharmacological treatment for major depressive disorder (MDD), therapeutic response varies, highlighting the need for molecular markers that predict treatment response, as well as insights into the biological processes underlying antidepressant efficacy. Candidate-gene and cross-sectional epigenome-wide association studies (EWAS) have reported DNA methylation signatures associated with antidepressant response; however, findings remain inconsistent. To date, no longitudinal EWAS has examined methylation trajectories across multiple time points during antidepressant treatment in MDD. Within the Early Medication Change trial, DNA methylation data was generated for 162 patients with MDD (81 responders, 81 non-responders) and 48 matched healthy controls. Patients were assessed at four times across eight-weeks of standardized antidepressant treatment, while controls were assessed twice. Differentially methylated positions (DMPs) and regions (DMRs) were identified using longitudinal EWAS models and the comb-p algorithm. Baseline methylation levels were associated with depressive symptom severity at day 28 and day 56 through two and six DMRs, respectively (e.g., TNRC6C, CAT), whereas no single DMP reached significance. Longitudinal analyses identified one DMP associated with improvement in depressive symptoms (YLMP1) and three DMRs showing methylation changes over time (e.g., GPR126, PM20D1). Combined patient-control analyses revealed additional DMPs and DMRs associated with diagnosis and temporal effects. This study provides first longitudinal evidence of regionally coordinated DNA methylation changes during antidepressant pharmacotherapy, revealing alterations in genes involved in neuroplasticity and inflammatory processes that are associated with clinical response. Replication and functional validation will be essential to determine their relevance for personalized antidepressant treatment.

### Immune-Microbiome Crosstalk in the Developmental Origins of Neurodevelopmental Disorders
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Biological Psychiatry (Biol Psychiatry) *(IF: 12.8)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר סקירה | **מחברים:** Plunk EC et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 3

Neurodevelopmental disorders (NDDs) arise from genetic and environmental factors converging across development, from embryogenesis through adulthood. Two systems central to brain development, the immune system and the microbiome, are incompletely formed at birth. Development of these immune and microbiome systems is shaped by factors transferred across prenatal and postnatal maternal-offspring interfaces including the placenta, birth canal, and breast. Despite literature linking each system independently to NDD risk, these fields have largely proceeded in parallel, rarely interrogating their interactions. This siloed approach constrains mechanistic understanding and progress. Here, we present evidence that the immune system and microbiome are interacting, not independent, influences on neurodevelopmental programming. Preclinical evidence from germ-free, antibiotic depletion, and maternal immune activation models demonstrates that perturbation of either system alters the other; human cohort evidence remains largely associational. Across each maternal-offspring interface, immune and microbial signals converge on the developing brain, acting on peripheral and neuroimmune populations. Microglia emerge as a recurring cellular mediator. Resolving mechanisms of NDD risk requires experimental designs that treat immune and microbial variables as interactive and that span prenatal through postnatal development. The field must move beyond disciplinary silos to integrate these systems across the full neurodevelopmental time course they jointly shape.

### Age-dependent structural brain aging in medication-free major depressive disorder: A multi-site analysis from COORDINATE-MDD consortium
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Biological Psychiatry (Biol Psychiatry) *(IF: 12.8)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Sharma B et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 3

BACKGROUND: Major depressive disorder (MDD) is associated with altered brain structure and accelerated brain aging. However, previous studies have been limited by clinical samples with mixed medication status and multiple mood states, modest sample sizes, underrepresentation of older adults with MDD, and/or reliance on summary-level data.

METHODS: Harmonized T1-weighted MRI from MDD (n = 645), all medication-free and in a current depressive episode, and matched healthy controls (n = 645), segmented into 145 regional volumes, from 11 sites in COORDINATE-MDD consortium. Brain age gap (BAG) was estimated cross-sectionally using gradient boosting regression with nested cross-validation. Group differences in BAG (and age-corrected BAG [cBAG]) were examined across age strata. Regional contributions were evaluated using SHAP values.

RESULTS: MDD was associated with significantly elevated cBAG compared with healthy controls (mean difference + 2.0 years [95% CI 1.251, 2.774], Cohen's d -0.289 [95% CI -0.399, -0.179]). Age-stratified analyses showed no differences before mid-30s, with progressively larger gaps thereafter, reaching +6.8 years in MDD aged 55 and older in unadjusted analyses (4.3 years after accounting for site). cBAG differed across neuroanatomical phenotypes previously identified using COORDINATE-MDD data. Key contributing regions included lateral and medial prefrontal regions, middle temporal gyrus, putamen, supplementary motor cortex, central operculum, and cerebellum.

CONCLUSIONS: Structural brain aging in MDD is age-dependent and most pronounced in a neuroanatomical phenotype associated with poorer key clinical outcomes. The findings are consistent with, but do not establish, neuroprogression models of MDD and demonstrate that cBAG is not a uniform feature of MDD, being more strongly expressed in a specifically clinically vulnerable disease phenotype.

### Harmonization of transdiagnostic psychiatric phenotypes across a large multi-site study reveals high burden of neuropsychiatric multimorbidity in 16p11.2 and 22q11.2 copy number variant carriers
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Biological Psychiatry (Biol Psychiatry) *(IF: 12.8)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Baribeau D et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 31

BACKGROUND: Modern psychiatric research is increasingly collaborative, but significant differences in recruitment and assessment methods across cohorts present major methodological challenges when integrating data.

METHODS: The Core Psychiatric Phenotype Summary (C2PS) tool was developed to harmonize data on psychiatric/neurodevelopmental diagnoses and subthreshold symptoms from multiple sources. Demonstrating its reliability, and breadth and depth, we report findings in the largest cohort to date of individuals with reciprocal copy number variants (CNVs) at 22q11.2 or 16p11.2, collected across 14 international sites in the Genes to Mental Health (G2MH) Network.

RESULTS: Neuropsychiatric phenotypes were harmonized for 1,325 G2MH participants (22qdel: n=752, 22qdup: 219, 16pdel: 227, 16pdup: 127, median age 16.0 years [IQR 11.0, 27.0], 52% female). Inter-rater reliability indicated fair-to-excellent agreement across C2PS domains (Light's Kappa: 0.48 to 1). Completion was feasible, with an average of 4% missingness (range 1% to 13%). Multimorbidity (> 2 disorders) affected 57%, with a median of 3 (IQR: 2 to 5) disorders per individual. Of those without any neuropsychiatric diagnosis (n= 244, 18%), subthreshold symptoms were common (82% in >1 domain, 57% > 2 domains). Higher multimorbidity was associated with lower adaptive functioning on the Vineland Adaptative Behavior Scales [-5.5 points per additional impacted domain (95% CI: -7.5, -3.6; p=5x10 -8 ].

CONCLUSIONS: The C2PS is a pragmatic and reliable tool to harmonize heterogeneous neuropsychiatric phenotypic data for large multi-national studies. Observations in 1,325 individuals with CNVs at 16p11.2 or 22q11.2 reveal variant-specific psychiatric profiles, emphasize burden and impact of psychiatric multimorbidity, and provide insights beyond categorical diagnoses.

### A Dynamic Model of Amygdala Circuits in Threat and Reward: Implications for Affective Disorders
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Biological Psychiatry (Biol Psychiatry) *(IF: 12.8)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר סקירה | **מחברים:** Zhu Y et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 3

Animals must continuously evaluate their surroundings and select adaptive responses to survive in ever-changing environments. Central to this capacity is the assignment of importance to environmental cues, which guides motivated behavior toward rewarding opportunities and away from potential threats. Here we propose a theoretical framework, a dynamic motivational model based on threat and reward imminence, in which motivational states evolve continuously as a function of imminence rather than shifting between discrete stages. Within this framework, three interacting dimensions define the stimulus relevance of an environmental cue: salience, valence, and value. We leverage the amygdala as a substrate to illustrate the circuit mechanisms underlying importance assignment and examine how stimulus imminence drives motivational state transitions to promote adaptive behavior. Applied to affective disorders, it reframes amygdala dysfunction as projection- and circuit-specific stress adaptation rather than uniform regional hyperactivity, providing a common language for comparing major depressive disorder, anxiety disorders, post-traumatic stress disorder, and substance use disorders. By integrating animal circuit studies with human neuroimaging and clinical evidence, this framework positions amygdala-centered networks as candidate biomarkers for biologically informed subtyping and targets for personalized treatment in depression and related affective disorders.

### Efficacy of Lumateperone 42 mg in Major Depressive Disorder: Subgroup Analysis of a Phase 3 Randomized, Placebo-Controlled Trial
**כתב עת:** 📄 Prim Care Companion CNS Disord | **סוג מחקר:** RCT (מחקר אקראי מבוקר) | **מחברים:** Durgam S et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 3

Objective:  Lumateperone is an FDA-approved atypical antipsychotic to treat schizophrenia, bipolar I or II depression, and major depressive disorder (MDD). Post hoc analyses of a randomized, phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (Study 501) evaluated the efficacy of lumateperone 42 mg adjunctive to antidepressant therapy (ADT) in demographic and clinical subgroups of patients with MDD with inadequate ADT response.  Methods:  Adults with  DSM-5  criteria for MDD with inadequate response to 1-2 courses of ADT, Montgomery- Åsberg Depression Rating Scale (MADRS) Total score ≥24, Clinical Global Impression Scale-Severity (CGI-S) score ≥4, and Quick Inventory of Depressive Symptomatology-Self-Report 16-item (QIDS-SR-16) score ≥14 were randomized 1:1 to 6-week oral lumateperone 42 mg+ADT or placebo+ADT. Efficacy was evaluated in the overall population and patient subgroups by demographic (age, sex, race, ethnicity, and region) and baseline disease characteristics (MADRS Total score, ADT type, number of ADT failures [in current episode and lifetime], and presence of anxious distress).  Results:  The modified intent-to-treat population comprised 481 patients (lumateperone+ADT, n = 239; placebo+ADT, n = 242). Lumateperone+ADT significantly improved MADRS Total score (least squares mean difference [LSMD], -4.9;  P  < .0001), CGI-S score (LSMD, -0.7;  P  < .0001), and QIDS-SR-16 Total score (LSMD, -2.4;  P  < .0001) at Day 43 versus placebo+ADT. Lumateperone+ADT significantly improved MADRS Total score ( P  < .01) across all demographic subgroups and baseline disease severity versus placebo+ADT. Significant improvements ( P  < .05) in CGI-S, QIDS-SR-16 Total scores, and MADRS response and remission rates were also observed across most subgroups.  Conclusion:  Lumateperone+ADT demonstrated robust efficacy over placebo+ADT across a broad range of demographic and clinical subgroups of patients with MDD.  Clinical Trials Registration . ClinicalTrials.gov identifier: NCT04985942.  Prim Care Companion CNS Disord   2026;28(6):26m04289 .  Author affiliations are listed at the end of this article.
