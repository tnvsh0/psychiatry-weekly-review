### Opposing excitatory and inhibitory synaptic pathologies drive ZC4H2-associated intellectual disability
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Neuron *(IF: 17.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Ma P et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 5

Understanding genotype-phenotype correlations is critical for precision medicine, yet patients carrying mutations in the same gene are often classified under one disorder and treated alike, despite potentially opposite responses to therapy. ZC4H2-associated rare disorder (ZARD) is an X-linked neurodevelopmental condition caused by diverse mutations across ZC4H2. Here, we show that distinct ZC4H2 mutations drive intellectual disability through opposing synaptic mechanisms. The gain-of-function mutation ZC4H2 L66H  enhances GABA A  receptor (GABA A R) degradation, impairing inhibitory synaptic transmission and plasticity. By contrast, the loss-of-function mutation ZC4H2 R213W  increases AMPA receptor (AMPAR) stability and dysregulates excitatory synaptic activity. Despite these diametrically opposed synaptic pathologies, ZC4H2 L66H/y  and ZC4H2 R213W/y  knockin mice exhibit comparable cognitive deficits. Importantly, muscimol, a GABA A R agonist, specifically rescues deficits in ZC4H2 L66H/y  mice, whereas the AMPAR antagonist perampanel rescues ZC4H2 R213W/y  mice. These findings demonstrate that opposing synaptic etiologies can underlie a single genetic disorder and highlight pathomechanism-based stratification for targeted therapeutic strategies.

### Hypothalamus-driven brain health through metabolic flexibility
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Neuron *(IF: 17.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר סקירה | **מחברים:** Horvath TL et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 3

Organismal physiology depends on continuous, non-hierarchical communication among cells, tissues, and organs. We argue that this communication rests on metabolic flexibility-the capacity of cells and tissues to adapt substrate use to changing energetic demands-and that the hypothalamus serves as the central hub coordinating this flexibility across the body. Using hypothalamic agouti-related protein (AgRP) and pro-opiomelanocortin (POMC) neurons as a model, we show these melanocortin neurons do more than regulate hunger and satiety: by integrating metabolic, hormonal, and immune signals, they shape reward, learning, executive function, and social behavior, while imposing coordinated metabolic states on peripheral tissues. When this flexibility is lost, as with aging or overnutrition, body-brain communication becomes rigid, driving cognitive decline, chronic inflammation, and neurodegenerative disease. We propose that signals from the body are not peripheral modifiers of brain function but fundamental determinants of it, with direct implications for hypothalamus-centered strategies to preserve brain health across the lifespan.

### Cell-specific transcription dysregulation in human Huntington's disease-positive developing striatum
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Brain *(IF: 14.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Precious SV et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 4

Huntington's disease (HD) is a neurodegenerative disorder caused by a CAG-repeat expansion in the HTT gene. Progressive loss of striatal projection neurons leads to cognitive, psychiatric, and motor impairments that typically manifest in midlife, despite the expansion being present from conception. Increasing evidence supports a neurodevelopmental component to HD; however, authentic human developing HD-positive striatal tissue has not previously been characterised. We used single-cell RNA sequencing to compare an HD-positive human fetal striatal sample to an age- and sex-matched control, with CAG-repeat lengths of 16, 49 and 16, 17 respectively. We also compared the fetal HD-positive transcriptional signature with publicly available datasets from postmortem adult HD-positive brain tissue. We identified 2,032 differentially expressed genes and defined nine cellular clusters.. Gene enrichment analysis revealed alteration of key biological processes across the developing HD-positive striatum, with pathway-level dysregulation varying between clusters. There was substantial overlap in gene expression changes between fetal and adult HD-positive striatal tissues, although not in all transcriptional profiles, and some were reversed. Together, these findings demonstrate that molecular features of HD pathology are present during early human striatal development, supporting the concept that disease mechanisms are established decades prior to clinical onset.

### Cell-type- and subregion-specific basolateral amygdala inputs to ventral CA1-subiculum
**כתב עת:** 🔷 The Journal of Neuroscience (J Neurosci) *(IF: 6.7)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Li 李明华 M et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 6

The hippocampus is essential for learning and memory and interacts extensively with cortical and subcortical regions. Although the heterogeneity of intrinsic connectivity of hippocampal neurons is well established, the diversity of its extrahippocampal connectivity remains less understood. Such differential connectivity may contribute to functional diversity of hippocampal neurons in specific positions. Ventral CA1 (vCA1) and ventral subiculum (vSub) form strong reciprocal connections with the basolateral amygdala (BLA), a region critical for emotional and social regulation. Consequently, BLA-vCA1-vSub circuits have been implicated in anxiety-related and social behaviors. However, the functional connectivity from BLA to vCA1-vSub at the cellular and circuit levels is underexplored. To address this, we combined viral tracing with Channelrhodopsin-2 (ChR2)-assisted slice electrophysiology to examine BLA projections to vCA1-vSub in mice of either sex. We found that BLA inputs are not uniformly distributed along the transverse axis in vCA1-vSub. Instead, BLA selectively excites pyramidal neurons in distal vCA1-vSub while largely avoiding proximal vCA1 near CA2. BLA terminals strongly excited parvalbumin-expressing interneurons but only weakly recruited a small subset of somatostatin-expressing interneurons, generating robust disynaptic inhibitory synaptic currents in pyramidal neurons. Additionally, the same distal pyramidal neurons that receive BLA excitation also project back to the BLA. These findings reveal that BLA establishes cell-type- and subregion-specific excitatory connections within vCA1-vSub, highlighting a circuit architecture that may underlie functional heterogeneity in ventral hippocampal processing. Significance statement  The hippocampus interacts extensively with cortical and subcortical regions, yet how long-range extrahippocampal inputs are organized across hippocampal subregions remains poorly understood. We examined projections from the basolateral amygdala (BLA) to the ventral hippocampus and found that BLA inputs selectively excite pyramidal neurons in distal ventral CA1 and ventral subiculum while largely avoiding proximal ventral CA1. BLA projections preferentially excite parvalbumin-expressing interneurons, provide disynaptic inhibition onto pyramidal neurons, and are reciprocally connected with the same pyramidal neurons in ventral CA1 and subiculum they excite. These findings reveal a highly selective connectivity pattern of amygdala-hippocampal circuits, providing a circuit framework for understanding how emotional information is routed through distinct ventral hippocampal pathways.
