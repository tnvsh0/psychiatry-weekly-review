### Long-term effectiveness of transdiagnostic cognitive-behavioural therapy for anxiety and depression in primary care: the PsicAP-CV trial
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Psychotherapy and Psychosomatics (Psychother Psychosom) *(IF: 15.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Muñoz-Navarro R et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 13

INTRODUCTION: Anxiety disorders and depression represent a global public health challenge due to their high prevalence, severely impacting quality of life (QoL) and functioning, and imposing a massive economic burden on public systems. This study evaluates the effectiveness of a group transdiagnostic cognitive behavioural therapy (TD-CBT) protocol through a stepped wedge cluster randomised controlled trial (SW-CRT) in Spanish primary care (PC).

METHODS: The sample consisted of 316 PC patients (73.4% female; mean age=44 years) with mild-to-moderate symptoms of anxiety and depression. An immediate intervention group was compared with a delayed intervention group (three months on a waiting list). Patients were assessed at pre-treatment, post-treatment, and 3- and 9-month follow-ups using mixed linear models.

RESULTS: Results revealed significant reductions in anxiety and depression symptoms with large effect sizes (d > 1.2) that remained stable up to 9 months. Improvements in QoL and functioning were heterogeneous, showing sustained long-term gains in work and family domains, but not in broader QoL or social functioning. No significant differences were found in clinical efficacy between groups, with solid reliable recovery rates (∼50%) and minimal deterioration. However, delayed access to treatment significantly increased long-term dropout rates (64.2% in the delayed group vs. 44.0% in the immediate group at 9 months).

CONCLUSIONS: In conclusion, group TD-CBT is an effective and safe intervention offering robust functional and symptomatic recovery. Crucially, while delayed treatment does not reduce clinical efficacy for completers, immediate access is essential to maximize patient retention and optimize resources in the public system.

### Dynamic change patterns in suicidal ideation differ in Brief Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (BCBT) and Present-Centered Therapy (PCT)
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Behaviour Research and Therapy (Behav Res Ther) *(IF: 5.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** RCT (מחקר אקראי מבוקר) | **מחברים:** Bryan CJ et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov

OBJECTIVE: Although Brief Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (BCBT) is more effective than Present-Centered Therapy (PCT) for preventing suicide attempts, the two treatments have similar efficacy for reducing suicidal ideation severity. Traditional severity scores may obscure meaningful differences in how specific dimensions of suicidal ideation actually change during treatment. This study examined how quickly and consistently different dimensions of suicidal ideation changed during BCBT and PCT.

METHOD: The first five items of the Scale for Suicidal Ideation (wish to live, wish to die, reasons for living versus dying, suicidal desire, and passive desire for death) were administered at every scheduled therapy session during a randomized clinical trial comparing BCBT to PCT among high-risk U.S. military personnel and veterans. We estimated separate continuous-time multivariate change models for each item and applied eigen-decomposition to the matrix of regression coefficients to characterize dominant change patterns.

RESULTS: In both treatments, some dimensions of suicidal ideation changed quickly while others changed slowly, and change was not evenly distributed across all five dimensions. Dimensions of suicidal ideation changed faster and in greater synchrony during BCBT than PCT.

CONCLUSIONS: Suicidal ideation reductions may arise through qualitatively different change processes across treatments even when the overall magnitude of change is similar. BCBT appeared to produce faster, more coordinated change across dimensions of suicidal ideation whereas PCT produced slower change that was less consistent across dimensions. These findings highlight the value of dynamical systems approaches for understanding mechanisms of therapeutic change that go beyond mean-level severity scores.

### The effects of induced yearning on attentional breadth of bereaved individuals: A cross-cultural study between China and Switzerland
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Behaviour Research and Therapy (Behav Res Ther) *(IF: 5.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Wen J et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 9

Bereaved individuals may experience persistent yearning for the deceased, marked by intense desire, mixed feelings, and recurring mental images of their loved one. Although the Attentional Scope Model links repetitive thinking to attentional breadth (i.e., the range of contextual information attended to at a given moment), how yearning influences attentional breadth across cultures remains unclear. We examined whether induced yearning would alter attentional breadth, whether this pattern differed for self- and other-related information, and whether these effects varied across cultural contexts and levels of grief-related vulnerability. In an online experiment, 398 bereaved adults from China (n = 236) and Switzerland (n = 162) completed baseline questionnaires and were randomly assigned to a loss-focused yearning condition or a separation-focused control condition in a yearning induction task. They then completed an attentional breadth task requiring them to detect targets presented either close to or far from a central self- or other-related word. More accurate detection of distant targets indicated broader attention. Results showed that attentional breadth differed by the interaction among cultural context, induction condition, word type, and target distance. Among Chinese participants in the yearning condition, higher trait yearning was associated with relatively broader attention around self-related than other-related information. In contrast, greater PGD symptom severity was associated with stronger attentional narrowing across cultures, but only in the control condition. These findings suggest that the effects of induced yearning on attentional breadth are context dependent. They offer insights into the underlying attentional processes involved and may inform culturally responsive interventions.

### Infant hippocampal and amygdala volume in an observational follow-up from a randomized clinical trial for maternal prenatal depression
**כתב עת:** 📄 Nat Commun | **סוג מחקר:** RCT (מחקר אקראי מבוקר) | **מחברים:** Demers CH et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct 16

Prenatal maternal depression may have intergenerational effects on offspring brain development, with potential socioemotional consequences. This observational follow-up study examines brain development and subsequent negative emotionality among 109 infants born to mothers participating in a randomized clinical trial (NCT03011801). In the prior trial, brief interpersonal psychotherapy significantly reduced prenatal depression symptoms and disorder as the published primary and secondary endpoints. Exploratory infant outcomes include hippocampal and amygdala volumes at 6 weeks and negative emotionality at 6 months. Here we show that infants of mothers randomized to interpersonal psychotherapy during pregnancy have lower right hippocampal volume than infants of mothers in the enhanced usual care group (p = .002, d = 0.62). No statistically significant group differences are observed in left hippocampal or amygdala volume. Smaller hippocampal volume predicts subsequent lower negative emotionality (r = 0.19, p = 0.045). In this study, a maternal prenatal depression intervention is associated with differences in infant brain development with implications for their subsequent emotional health.

### Acceptance and Commitment Therapy as a Psychological Intervention for Primary Caregivers of Patients With Decompensated Cirrhosis: A Randomized Controlled Trial
**כתב עת:** 📄 Int J Nurs Pract | **סוג מחקר:** RCT (מחקר אקראי מבוקר) | **מחברים:** Ding Y et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Oct

AIM: This study aimed to evaluate the effects of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, relative to usual care, on psychological flexibility, resilience, caregiving burden, anxiety and depression in primary caregivers of patients with decompensated cirrhosis.

BACKGROUND: Primary caregivers of decompensated cirrhosis patients face high rates of anxiety and depression due to long-term caregiving stress, financial burdens and disease uncertainty. Acceptance and Commitment Therapy improves mental health in other chronic disease caregivers, but its effects on cirrhosis caregivers in China remain unstudied.

DESIGN: This was a randomized, assessor-blinded, parallel-group trial comparing Acceptance and Commitment Therapy-enhanced care with routine care alone in primary caregivers during hospitalization.

METHOD: A total of 120 caregivers were recruited at a Chinese hospital (January-April 2023) and randomly allocated to control (routine care) or intervention (routine care plus six-session Acceptance and Commitment Therapy) groups. Validated scales assessed psychological flexibility, resilience, burden, anxiety and depression at admission, discharge and 1 month postdischarge. Data were analysed using chi-square tests, independent t-tests for baseline comparisons and repeated-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni post hoc tests for pairwise comparisons.

RESULTS: Compared to controls, the intervention group showed significantly lower scores in caregiving burden, anxiety, depression and psychological rigidity, alongside higher psychological resilience at discharge and 1-month follow-up. No adverse events were recorded in either group.

CONCLUSION: In this single-centre trial, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy reduced psychological rigidity and caregiving burden and improved psychological resilience. Its emphasis on accepting uncontrollable realities and acting on personal values may be well suited to the volatile context of cirrhosis caregiving, but multicentre trials with longer follow-up are needed before wider implementation.

TRIAL REGISTRATION: Chinese Clinical Trial Registry: ChiCTR2600131432.
