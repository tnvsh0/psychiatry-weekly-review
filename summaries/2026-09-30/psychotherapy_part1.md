### Emotion beliefs predict resilience via emotional reactivity: A multi-timepoint study in university students
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Behaviour Research and Therapy (Behav Res Ther) *(IF: 5.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Notebaert L et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Sep 15

Understanding resilience in tertiary students is important, as this developmental period is characterised by elevated stressor exposure and vulnerability to mental health difficulties. Although emotion beliefs and emotional reactivity are each linked to mental health, little is known about their role in resilient functioning following adversity. This study used a three-wave longitudinal design in undergraduate university students (N = 112) to examine whether maladaptive emotion beliefs predict resilient functioning and whether these associations operate through positive and negative emotional reactivity. Emotion beliefs were assessed at baseline (T1), while emotional reactivity, stressor exposure, and mental health were assessed at two follow-up timepoints (T2 and T3). Resilient functioning was indexed relative to stressor exposure, with higher scores indicating better-than-expected emotional functioning. Results showed that positive emotional reactivity was positively associated with resilient functioning, whereas negative emotional reactivity was negatively associated with resilient functioning. Stronger beliefs that emotions are uncontrollable were associated with poorer resilient functioning overall, with some evidence of an indirect pathway through greater negative emotional reactivity. No corresponding associations were observed for beliefs about the usefulness of emotions. However, associations involving emotion beliefs were no longer significant after accounting for baseline mental health. The pattern of structural relationships did not differ significantly across follow-up assessments. These findings highlight emotional reactivity as an important correlate of resilient functioning and provide preliminary evidence that beliefs about emotion controllability may also be relevant, although their unique contribution beyond pre-existing mental health requires further investigation.

### The establishment of primal repression and its consequences in contemporary clinical practice
**כתב עת:** 📄 The International Journal of Psychoanalysis (Int J Psychoanal) *(IF: 1.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Artaloytia JF | **תאריך:** 2026 Aug

Freud himself never clearly and unequivocally defined his concept of primal repression, which has been considered mythical, obscure, mysterious, incomplete, enigmatic or controversial. This paper revisits the concept with the support of the hypothetical conceptual clarity provided by the heard word (soundimage) and the visual image discussed in Freud's book On Aphasia: A critical Study (1953[1891]). The heard word allows for affective change and reorganisation according to Oedipal law, which is postulated as the essence of primal repression. Thing-presentation takes place prior to word-presentation. The word would retrospectively generate two elements that the author calls the Ding- and the Sach- elements of the thing-presentation. An everyday scene is used to illustrate the theoretical approach. Three vignettes are subsequently examined: a neurotic patient's dream to illustrate how primal repression works when it has been adequately established; a contrasting clinical experience with a schizophrenic patient in which the opposite is true; and finally, an intermediate situation is presented, in which the analyst's primal repression of filicide is fundamental to resolve an enactment. The latter two situations also illustrate the role of supporting the generation of primal repression in contemporary analytical work.

### The construction of masculinity in Selk'nam culture. A psychoanalytic understanding of the initiation ceremony
**כתב עת:** 📄 The International Journal of Psychoanalysis (Int J Psychoanal) *(IF: 1.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Rivera G, Santander P | **תאריך:** 2026 Aug

In this article, a psychoanalytic analysis of the Hain ceremony of the Selk'nam people is undertaken with the aim of reflecting on the construction of masculinity and the threats underlying it. This ceremony, which is central to the transition from childhood to adulthood in the culture, is based on a myth chronicling the existence of a prior matriarchy. The fear of the return of this regime gives rise to a ceremony symbolising a counter-response, sustained by a pact of silence among men. The underlying threat is rooted in the fear of female domination and submission to women. It is suggested that this myth contains a specific truth connected to contact with vulnerability. It is proposed that the combination of an intense mother-son relationship, coupled with distance from the father during the early stages of development, is connected to the threat of regression to a mother-child bonding state as a form of protection from the anxieties associated with facing the world. At the same time, this generates the need to establish defensive barriers towards women.

### The effectiveness of digital therapeutics in improving sleep quality: a systematic review and network meta-analysis
**כתב עת:** 📄 BMC Med | **סוג מחקר:** מטה-אנליזה | **מחברים:** Tong Z et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Sep 28

BACKGROUND: Insomnia is a prevalent global sleep disorder. Digital therapeutics, delivered via online platforms, offer novel intervention options. However, existing systematic reviews largely focus on validating individual digital therapeutics, with limited comparative evidence across different types within a unified network. This network meta-analysis aims to evaluate the comparative effectiveness of various digital therapeutics on sleep quality, thereby providing evidence-based guidance for clinical decision-making in insomnia management.

METHODS: PubMed, Cochrane Library, Embase, and Web of Science were systematically searched and included eligible randomized controlled trials (RCTs). Outcomes included the Insomnia Severity Index (ISI), the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI), sleep diary and objective sleep parameters. Sleep diary parameters included subjective total sleep time (sTST), subjective wake after sleep onset (sWASO), subjective sleep onset latency (sSOL), and subjective sleep efficiency (sSE); objective sleep parameters included total sleep time (TST), wake after sleep onset (WASO), sleep efficiency (SE), and sleep onset latency (SOL). Furthermore, the minimal clinically important difference (MCID) was employed to evaluate the clinical relevance of all outcomes.

RESULTS: A total of 96 RCTs comprising 18,419 participants were included, assessing 6 digital therapeutics: digital cognitive behavioral therapy (dCBT), digital brief behavioral therapy (dBBT), digital mindfulness-based therapy (dMBT), circadian rhythm support (CRS), virtual reality (VR), and tele-neurofeedback (NFB). Quality assessment of the included studies revealed that 2 low risk, 79 some concerns, 15 high risk. Network meta-analysis indicated that, compared with control treatment (CT), dCBT likely resulted in a significant decrease in ISI score (MD: -4.24 points, 95% CI: -4.83 to - 3.65; moderate-certainty evidence), a reduction in PSQI score (MD: -2.28 points, 95% CI: -2.88 to - 1.68; low-certainty evidence), shortened sSOL (MD: -12.57 min, 95% CI: -15.14 to - 10.00; low-certainty evidence), improved sSE (MD: 7.27%, 95% CI: 5.77 to 8.77; low-certainty evidence), and reduced sWASO (MD: -17.19 min, 95% CI: -20.96 to - 13.41; low-certainty evidence). dMBT significantly reduced ISI score (MD: -2.28 points, 95% CI: -4.00 to - 0.55; low-certainty evidence) and shortened SOL (MD: -7.96 min, 95% CI: -15.50 to - 0.42; moderate-certainty evidence). Based on an inconsistency model, VR substantially reduced WASO (MD: -13.38 min, 95% CI: -24.40 to - 2.36; moderate-certainty evidence). Of these findings, only dCBT reached the MCID for ISI improvement. NFB, dBBT, and CRS did not demonstrate statistically significant effects relative to CT for any of the outcomes. Given the constraints in the number of studies and sample sizes, the findings derived from objective outcomes warrant cautious interpretation.

CONCLUSIONS: Digital therapeutics constitute a promising intervention for enhancing sleep quality. dCBT optimally reduces insomnia severity; other outcomes are statistically significant but fail to reach the MCID, leaving their clinical significance uncertain. Moreover, the majority of effect estimates derived from head-to-head comparisons among different digital therapeutics remain insufficiently precise. Future studies are warranted to confirm and expand upon these findings through large-scale, high-quality RCTs.

### A comparison of the efficacy of cognitive-behavioral stress management and acceptance and commitment therapy on the quality of life and fatigue of caregivers of patients with cancer: a randomized controlled trial
**כתב עת:** 📄 Support Care Cancer | **סוג מחקר:** RCT (מחקר אקראי מבוקר) | **מחברים:** Hosseini FA et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Sep 28

PURPOSE: This study sought to compare the effectiveness of cognitive behavioral stress management (CBSM) and acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT) on the quality of life and fatigue of caregivers of cancer patients.

METHOD: This single-site, parallel-group, three-arm, superiority randomized controlled trial with a 1:1:1 allocation ratio includes 66 caregivers of cancer patients who were randomly assigned to either the CBSM, ACT, or control group. The CBSM and ACT interventions each consisted of eight in-person sessions, conducted twice a week for 90 min each. Participants in all three groups completed the Caregiver Quality of Life Index-Cancer and the Multidimensional Fatigue Inventory in all pre, post, and follow-up phases.

RESULTS: A significant difference was observed in the quality of life and caregiver fatigue among the three groups in both the posttest and follow-up phases (p < 0.05), which were maintained 2 months after the intervention, apart from quality of life in the CBSM group. However, there was no statistically significant difference in the variables between the two interventions (p > 0.05).

CONCLUSION: The effectiveness of both ACT and CBSM in enhancing caregivers' quality of life and alleviating fatigue offers a promising perspective for the development and implementation of supportive and therapeutic programs aimed at enhancing the mental health of caregivers of cancer patients.
