### Effects of Cognitive-Behavioral Treatment in Children With Affective Dysregulation (ADOPT-Treatment): A Randomized Controlled Trial
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry (J Am Acad Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 10.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Treier AK et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 12

OBJECTIVE: Affective dysregulation (AD) in childhood is characterized by heightened reactivity to emotional stimuli and impairments across multiple domains. Evidence on the efficacy of psychosocial interventions specifically tailored to AD is scarce.

METHOD: In this multicenter randomized controlled trial, 159 children aged 8-12 years with AD were randomized to either a modular cognitive-behavioral intervention (THOPAS; 24 sessions) or treatment as usual (TAU). Primary outcome was clinician-rated AD symptom severity (blinded and composite), and secondary outcomes were functional impairment, co-occurring conditions, emotion regulation, and parenting. Assessments were conducted at baseline, post-intervention, and 32-week follow-up. The analyses followed modified intention-to-treat and per-protocol principles.

RESULTS: No between-group differences emerged for the primary outcome, and only isolated effects were found for secondary outcomes. Both THOPAS and TAU showed small-to-moderate improvements over time. Exploratory subgroup analyses suggested that children with higher baseline symptom severity may have benefited more from THOPAS. Feasibility indicators suggested high therapist fidelity, strong parental engagement, high satisfaction, and high safety for THOPAS.

CONCLUSION: The trial found no overall superiority of THOPAS over high-quality TAU. While THOPAS was feasible and well-received, the study was not powered to detect small effects. Future research should incorporate both standardized and individualized outcome measures, refine baseline severity thresholds, and examine the long-term stability of treatment effects.

CLINICAL TRIAL REGISTRATION INFORMATION: Efficacy of a Personalized Modular Outpatient Treatment of Affective Dysregulation and Coexisting Conditions in Children (ADOPT Treatment); https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00013317.

### Longitudinal patterns of attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder medication use and subsequent incident substance use among US youth
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry (J Child Psychol Psychiatry) *(IF: 7.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Evans-Polce RJ et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 13

BACKGROUND: Understanding potential substance use risks or protection associated with ADHD medication use over time among US youth with ADHD has clinical and public health importance. Using longitudinal nationally representative data and accounting for pre-existing differences in youth can shed light on these associations.

METHODS: This study used longitudinal prospective data from the Population Assessment of Tobacco and Health Study (2013/14-2023). Analyses included youth ages 12-17 with a parent-reported history of an ADHD diagnosis by a doctor, nurse, or health professional (n = 4,700 observations; n = 2,140 unique individuals). Using propensity score weighting to account for pre-exposure differences, the effect of ADHD medication initiation compared to no ADHD medication use and the effect of ADHD medication discontinuation compared to continuation on five incident substance use outcomes (alcohol, nicotine/tobacco, e-cigarettes, cannabis, other drugs) 1 year later were examined.

RESULTS: Among youth with parent-reported ADHD, those who initiated ADHD medication did not differ from youth who did not use ADHD medication in subsequent incident substance use risk (adjusted odds ratios ranged from 0.94 to 1.36). Similarly, those who discontinued ADHD medication did not differ from youth who continued ADHD medication in subsequent risk for incident substance use (adjusted odds ratios ranged from 0.98 to 1.02).

CONCLUSIONS: These results add to the evidence that ADHD medication use initiation and medication discontinuation patterns are not related to an increased risk for incident substance use among US youth. This study highlights the importance of considering baseline differences between youth when considering the effect of ADHD medication on future substance use risk.

### Functional brain connectivity in patients with anorexia nervosa before and after short-term weight restoration
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry (J Child Psychol Psychiatry) *(IF: 7.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Geisler D et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 9

BACKGROUND: Resting-state functional connectivity (RSFC) studies have consistently identified widespread alterations in the brain's functional network architecture in anorexia nervosa (AN), but it remains unclear whether they are reversible with weight restoration. Furthermore, it is not clear whether there are associations between RSFC and neuroendocrine changes, such as leptin suppression in the underweight state of AN and its increase during short-term weight restoration.

METHODS: We first analyzed RSFC data cross-sectionally in young female patients with AN (n = 117) and healthy control participants (n = 117) using network-based statistic (NBS) and graph theory metrics and subsequently explored relationships with plasma leptin as a marker of undernutrition. A subset of patients (n = 73) was reassessed longitudinally after short-term weight restoration [mean body mass index (BMI) increase: 28.0%] to test for changes in identified alterations.

RESULTS: NBS revealed increased RSFC in cingulo-occipito-cerebellar circuitry in underweight patients with AN (p = .0018), which decreased over the inpatient weight restoration interval (p < .001). This change correlated negatively with plasma leptin concentrations (p = .042), and plasma leptin appeared to partially mediate the association between BMI increase and RSFC decrease (p = .039), suggesting a possible region-specific modulatory effect. Underweight patients with AN also showed higher characteristic path length (p = .035), indicating reduced integration between brain regions, and higher assortativity (p = .013), indicating stronger coupling among similarly connected regions. Longitudinally, characteristic path length decreased during weight restoration (p = .004), whereas findings for assortativity were suggestive of no change.

CONCLUSIONS: The results provide novel longitudinal evidence that functional connectome alterations in AN partially normalize during short-term weight restoration. Speculatively, taking previous results in long-term recovered individuals into consideration, higher assortativity might reflect potential vulnerability for AN. Our finding of a tentative association between the normalization of plasma leptin concentrations and RSFC suggests the possibility that leptin may modulate changes in specific patterns of functional connectivity, offering a potential neurobiological target for novel pharmacotherapies.

### Does early childhood education and care for infants and toddlers cause behavior problems up to age 8?
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry (J Child Psychol Psychiatry) *(IF: 7.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Zachrisson HD et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 13

BACKGROUND: The causal effect of attending Early Childhood Education and Care (ECEC) for infants and toddlers on child behavior problems is contested. Yet, most previous research is correlational. Findings from previous research are mixed, but with some evidence of harmful effects on children's behavior. Some correlational studies also suggest that ECEC may be most harmful for children from socioeconomically advantaged families.

METHODS: We estimate the effect of age of entry into ECEC on externalizing behavior problems at ages 3, 5, and 8 years in a large sample (n = 66,394) from The Norwegian Mother, Father, and Child Cohort study (MoBa). We use two quasi-experimental methods, Two-way fixed-effects models and instrumental variable models, that exploit variations in how early families enrolled their children in ECEC due to a national scale-up of publicly funded centers.

RESULTS: In this high-powered, rigorous approach to testing the causal impact of age of entry into ECEC, we find no evidence that entry around age 1 relative to later affects behavior problems either during preschool years (at ages 3 or 5) or after school entry (at age 8 years). We also find no evidence that these effects vary by parental education or family income.

CONCLUSIONS: We find no evidence suggesting that early entry into ECEC has harmful consequences for children's behavior regulation during the preschool years or after school entry. We argue that our null results may be due to ECEC pedagogy in Norway that emphasizes young children's well-being and playful learning experiences.

### Measurement invariance of the affective reactivity index across five countries
**כתב עת:** 🔷 European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry (Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 6.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Silver J et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 10

OBJECTIVE: Irritability is a globally relevant dimension of youth psychopathology, yet cross-national comparisons require evidence that assessment tools function equivalently across countries and languages. We examined the reliability, factor structure, and measurement invariance of the Affective Reactivity Index (ARI) across samples from Brazil, China, Japan, Spain, and the United States (US).

METHOD: Community samples (N = 6,195; ages 6-18 years) completed parent- or youth-report ARI versions. Single-factor confirmatory factor analyses evaluated model fit and internal consistency. Multi-group CFA tested measurement invariance. Differences in observed total scores between samples and pooled age and sex associations were examined using linear regression.

RESULTS: Internal consistency was acceptable to good across country samples and informants. A single-factor structure was broadly supported. For parent report, measurement invariance was well supported across China and Japan using the original three-category format and also supported across China, Japan, and the US after dichotomization of all items, due to lack of endorsement of the highest category of Item 4 in the US sample. For youth report, threshold and loading invariance were supported, but scalar invariance was not. Parent-reported irritability was higher in the Chinese and Japanese samples than in the U.S. sample, whereas youth-reported irritability was highest in the Brazilian sample. Parents reported higher irritability in boys and younger youth; youth self-report showed no significant age or sex effects.

CONCLUSION: The ARI showed broadly comparable measurement properties across the participating samples, with stronger evidence for parent report than youth report. However, parent-report comparisons involving the US were based on a dichotomized response format, and full intercept invariance was not established for youth report. Observed score differences should therefore be interpreted cautiously, and as differences between participating samples rather than population-level cross-national differences.

### Behavioural parent training for disruptive behaviour in children with autism: moderators and mediators of the effects of a randomized controlled trial
**כתב עת:** 🔷 European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry (Eur Child Adolesc Psychiatry) *(IF: 6.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Woelk M et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 9

Behavioural parent training is a well-established intervention for children with autism and disruptive behaviour. In a recent randomized controlled trial on parent training for children with autism and disruptive behaviour, we found positive effects of face-to-face (but not blended) parent training on children's noncompliance and irritability, and on parenting sense of competence and overreactive parenting. However, effect sizes were medium, which may indicate room for improvement. Here, we investigated two potential avenues to achieve such improvement: (1) by getting a better understanding of the mechanisms of change of the intervention (i.e., mediation effects); (2) by identifying whether there are subgroups for whom the intervention works better or worse (i.e., moderation effects). We tested whether parenting sense of competence, parenting stress and parenting style mediated the effects of parent training on our primary outcome measures child noncompliance and irritability. In addition, we examined if parenting sense of competence, parenting stress, parenting style, parental attitudes or parental psychopathology moderated treatment success. We found that an improvement in parenting sense of competence was associated with the effect of face-to-face parent training on children's noncompliance, but not on irritability, while changes in parenting stress or parenting style were not related to treatment effects. We did not find any moderating effects on the effects of parent training. We conclude that, based on these results, more focus on increasing parents' feelings of competence as a parent might be a way forward to increase the effectiveness of behavioural parent training for children with autism and disruptive behaviour.
