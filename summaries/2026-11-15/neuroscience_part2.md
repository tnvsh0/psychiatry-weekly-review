### Information formatting in neuronal populations predicts differential functional contributions to behavior
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Nature Neuroscience (Nat Neurosci) *(IF: 25.0)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Ruff DA et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 10

Neurons throughout the brain encode essentially everything, so why have distinct areas? We proposed that areas format different information sources differently, implying their computational role. Here we compared the middle temporal (MT) area, involved in visual motion perception, and the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (dlPFC), implicated in decision-making and reward. In a task that required monkeys to combine motion and reward information, both regions encoded both motion and reward, but formatted differently. Area MT represented them separably, whereas the dlPFC combined them in a decision-related format. A recurrent network model trained to reproduce that formatting predicted a distinct effect of stimulating those areas. Consistent with predictions, MT stimulation biased choices whereas dlPFC stimulation produced 'winner-take-all' outcomes. Our findings suggest that a modular structure flexibly reformats information to enable complex behaviors.

### An astrocyte-microglia MFG-E8 axis promotes synapse engulfment in early amyloid pathology
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Neuron *(IF: 17.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Sokolova D et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 9

Region-specific synapse loss is an early hallmark of Alzheimer's disease, yet the upstream signals that render synapses vulnerable remain unclear. In a mouse model of amyloidosis, we identify an early astrocyte state that emerges in vulnerable brain regions before widespread plaque deposition and overt gliosis. These astrocytes exhibit bulbous-looking perisynaptic processes enriched in p62 and glycogen, impaired autophagic flux and calcium signaling, and diminished astrocyte-synapse coverage. Rather than exhibiting increased synapse engulfment, bulbous astrocytes show elevated milk fat globule-epidermal growth factor 8 (MFG-E8) and are associated with increased local microglial synapse engulfment. Astrocyte-specific gain- and loss-of-function experiments demonstrate that astrocyte-derived MFG-E8 is required for microglial synapse engulfment and acts through microglial β3 integrin signaling. Astrocytic Mfge8 deletion reduces microglial engulfment and synapse loss across amyloidosis models and is associated with reduced amyloid pathology, gliosis, and cognitive impairment. Together, these findings define an astrocyte-microglia MFG-E8 signaling axis that contributes to region-specific synaptic vulnerability during early amyloid pathology.

### Neural organoid models of human brain evolution
**כתב עת:** ⭐ Neuron *(IF: 17.2)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר סקירה | **מחברים:** Day CF et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 11

The human brain has structural and functional differences to our closest living relatives, the other great apes. It is thought that these differences give rise to many of the traits we associate with being human, such as our prodigious capacity to learn and be creative. However, the molecular mechanisms that underpin these differences are mostly unknown. In this review, we describe how neural organoids, an in vitro model of brain development, enable questions of human brain evolution to be functionally investigated in evolutionarily relevant developmental contexts. We discuss new insights at the genetic, cellular, and developmental scales and highlight current challenges in organoid generation, in long-term culture, and in establishing multimodal phenotypic measurements. We anticipate expanding the application of organoid models across Mammalia to help us better define species- and lineage-specific traits and improve our understanding of how complex traits emerge and evolve across developmental and evolutionary timescales.

### Expanding the genetic landscape of moyamoya angiopathy: insights from 74 trios
**כתב עת:** 🔷 Brain *(IF: 14.5)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Aloui C et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 11

Moyamoya angiopathy (MMA) is a rare cerebrovascular disorder characterized by progressive stenosis of intracranial arteries and the development of abnormal collateral vessels. While RNF213 is a major susceptibility gene in East Asian populations, the genetic architecture of MMA beyond RNF213, particularly in Europeans, remains largely undefined beyond a few known syndromic causes. We performed whole-exome sequencing on 74 sporadic MMA trios (proband and unaffected parents) of predominantly European ancestry. An additional cohort of 200 probands was screened for gene recurrence. We applied a stringent filtering strategy to identify de novo and rare inherited variants, followed by gene prioritization based on structural modeling, expression data, and biological plausibility. Gene-based burden tests were conducted against large control databases (gnomAD). We identified causative variants in 20 of the 74 probands (27%). Six probands with severe syndromic infant-onset phenotypes harbored de novo variants in the E3 ligase domain of RNF213. Fourteen probands carried a causative copy number variant (n = 3) or a short variant (n = 11) in a known MMA-associated gene (CBL, NF1, PTPN11, DIAPH1, ANO1, JAG1, NOS3, HBB). Beyond these established genes, we identified strong candidate variants in 11 probands from the discovery cohort, involving 11 novel candidate genes. Screening the additional cohort of 200 unrelated MMA probands identified recurrence of the gain-of-function SMAD4 p.Arg496Cys variant, which was de novo in the discovery-cohort proband, linking MMA to dysregulated TGF-β signaling. Other candidate findings included de novo loss-of-function variants in genes regulating epigenetic modulation and RNA splicing (SETD2, HNRNPL, UBAP2L, TOP2B, PRPF40A) and variants affecting vascular stress response (EPAS1, SVEP1, PDCD4). RNF213 was the only gene reaching exome-wide significance in burden testing (P = 2.11 × 10⁻11). This study establishes that MMA in non-East Asian populations is characterized by a strong genetic heterogeneity. These findings expand the molecular landscape of MMA and highlight the utility of trio-based sequencing for uncovering very rare molecular players in this disease. Additional analyses will now be needed to investigate the convergence of MMA pathways.

### Subliminal beauty engages the brain's valuation circuits
**כתב עת:** 🔷 The Journal of Neuroscience (J Neurosci) *(IF: 6.7)* | **סוג מחקר:** מאמר מחקרי | **מחברים:** Fernandes P et al. | **תאריך:** 2026 Nov 9

Neuroeconomic models propose that the anterior ventral striatum (aVS) and ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC) are key regions for computing subjective value (SV) signals that guide choice. However, the role of conscious awareness in this process remains debated. Here we examined whether SV can be automatically computed in these regions without conscious awareness. In an fMRI experiment, human participants (24 females, 5 males) viewed faces that varied in attractiveness under three conditions: (i) suppressed from awareness via continuous flash suppression (CFS), (ii) clearly visible without suppression, or (iii) absent (background only) with CFS. Participants reported trial-wise facial identity (objective) and visibility (subjective) measures of facial awareness. In a post-fMRI session, they rated the attractiveness of each face as a measure of SV. When faces were seen, task performance (d') exceeded chance and responses were faster than in absent trials. When faces were unseen, performance was at chance but responses were slower than absent trials. Neurally, seen and unseen faces elicited greater neural signal than absent trials and showed similar neural patterns in the fusiform face area (FFA). Critically, neural signal in vmPFC correlated with SV for both seen and unseen faces, with similar neural patterns. In aVS, SV-related signal was only observed for unseen faces. Furthermore, mean face-related signal in FFA correlated with SV-related signals in aVS and vmPFC for unseen faces. These findings demonstrate that SV can be automatically computed in aVS and vmPFC without conscious awareness, suggesting a neural pathway by which subliminal information can influence value-based choice. Significance statement  A central question in consciousness research and decision neuroscience is whether the brain's valuation circuitry - particularly the anterior ventral striatum (aVS) and ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC) - can compute subjective value automatically without conscious awareness. This question is important because prevailing accounts often assume that cognitive functions supported by the prefrontal cortex require conscious awareness of task-relevant information. Using continuous flash suppression and neuroimaging, we found that faces reported unseen nevertheless evoked value-related signals in aVS and vmPFC that scaled with subjective attractiveness ratings. These findings demonstrate that subjective value can be computed automatically from social information in the absence of conscious awareness.
